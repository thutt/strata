THE CLAIMS OF THE CHALLENGE DOCUMENT
====================================

The document "How do your document preparation systems fare?" is made from
manual/src/challenge.src by "manual/build.sh challenge" (the PDF is
challenge.pdf of the manual folder of the build).  It is part of "build.sh
all" and of the install: the PDF file with the other manuals, and the
document manual/challenge/challenge.doc (a committed file, made by "build.sh
publish") in the folder share/strata/doc/challenge of the install, with its
pictures in a folder next to it.  This file says where every statement of it
comes from.  tests/challenge.py checks that the numbers of
the document are the numbers of section 7 of docs/BIG-DOCUMENTS.txt, which
gives the commands.

The rules (the owner's, kept in docs/WORKLIST.txt):

  1. Only what was measured on Strata is stated, with the machine and the
     commands that give the numbers.
  2. No number about another program, unless the owner measures it.
  3. A claim about a competitor must be checked against its own public
     documentation, must say what that program can do, and must not say that
     it lacks a feature that it has.
  4. The document names no program of the original product and no
     reference to it.  It says Editor, Document Manager, Printer, Viewer.

How the rules were kept.  The document makes no statement about any other
program, and names none: rules 2 and 3 are kept by saying nothing.  It states
the challenge as questions (section "The questions") and says what was
measured for Strata.  If a later version names another program, the feature
and the page of its documentation where it was checked must be written into
this file first, and tests/challenge.py has a list of names (OTHER_NAMES)
that fails until then.

Where each number comes from (all of them: docs/BIG-DOCUMENTS.txt, section 7).

  The document of the manual   "build.sh single", release edition: 724 pages
                               (pdfinfo), 1,910,286 bytes (ls), items and
                               the identical Saves (bigtest, roundtrip),
                               headlines, index entries and figures (the
                               records H, I and F of the .meta file).
  The machine                  lscpu, free and "pdftex --version" of the
                               virtual machine.
  The Editor tables            bigtest (without a terminal) and
                               tests/bigtui.py (in a pseudo-terminal), run
                               on the release build.
  The Printer table            strprint (the whole manual, two passes, the
                               DVI route), stred --print (the parts), "time
                               -v" for the memory, "strata-build manual" for
                               the manual set, "strata-build big" for the
                               synthetic document of 1,312 pages.
  The rate                     724 pages in 65.2 s: 11.1 pages every second
                               (the quotient, in the document as an equation).
  The Viewer                   tests/big.py (viewer), run in a debug build,
                               which is slower than a release build; the
                               document says so.

Where each statement about a feature comes from (the pictures and the pages
of the manual are in manual/challenge/pictures, pages.txt there says which
page of the PDF each picture is).

  Equations are TeX math in a marker, typeset by the Printer, drawn on the
  screen by the Editor        manual chapter 9 (the page of the picture:
                               ch-equation).
  A cross-reference is a marker that names its target; the number or page is
  made when the document is printed
                               manual/mkmanual.py (the header: the contents,
                               the index and the list of figures are tables
                               of cross-reference markers) and the chapter on
                               the Cross-reference marker.
  The pages of the contents, the index and the list of figures are checked
  against the PDF            manual/checkpdf.py (build.sh check-edition) and
                               tests/big.py (the synthetic document).
  Numbered sections, an outline to a level, Go To a section, Autosort
                               chapter 3 of the manual (Edit) and the
                               commands that bigtest times.
  Tables with borders         the tables on the page of the picture
                               ch-table (section 5.38 of the
                               manual) and the Border panel of the chapter
                               Edit.
  Keep blocks                 section "Keep" of the chapter Markers and the
                               table of the Keep markers of the chapter
                               Print and Preview (build rule: a region
                               stays on one page).
  Print Error markers and the Error Log
                               the chapters Markers and Print and Preview
                               (the picture ch-errors is a copy of
                               doc/pictures/markers-errors.png, taken by
                               manual/scenes/errlog.py from the Editor).
  Line numbering, in the Preview and in the PDF alike
                               docs/LINENUMBERING-LATEX.txt and
                               tests/lineno.py; the picture
                               ch-numbered is page 1 of
                               samples/letters/complaint-to-utility-us.doc
                               printed with the record FZM1.

Left out on purpose.

  UTF-8 as a feature point: the item "UTF-8 instead of the code page" of
  docs/WORKLIST.txt is an exploration and is not complete.
  Statements about Microsoft Word, Google Docs or any other program.
  Numbers of the older machine (docs/BIG-DOCUMENTS.txt sections 2 to 6): they
  belong to a manual of 753 pages and to another machine.

The pictures.  The Editor with the whole manual open, and its outline, were
taken by manual/challenge/shots.py from the X11 build on the virtual display,
with the Reference Manual of this release open.  Four pictures are pages of
the PDF of the manual (or of a sample) drawn by pdftoppm.  They are sources
kept in git; shots.py is run on purpose, never by a build.
