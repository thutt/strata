A KEYCAP THAT CANNOT BE DRAWN IS DRAWN AS A MARKER
===================================================

Status: implemented in the X11 version of the Editor.  The terminal version
does not draw pictures and is not changed.

1. What the owner saw
---------------------
In the Reference Manual (owner copy of 21 November 2026) the passage that
says that a key written with a capital letter after Ctrl- or Alt- is the same
key whether or not Shift is down showed three keycaps as a gap the size of a
keycap with nothing in it: Ctrl-s, the second Ctrl-S and the second F12.  The
other keycaps of the page were drawn.  The owner's comment: a keycap that is
not displayed because LaTeX returned an error must be drawn as a marker.

2. The cause
------------
It was not LaTeX, the case of the legends, a race or a stopped job.  The cache
folder held a picture (.png) for each of the 17 different legends of the
passage and no .err file.  The Editor passes the pictures of a screen to the
display in a list (tb_img of src/termbuf.c) that held 16 pictures; the 17th
and later were dropped without a message, while their cells in the line had
been reserved for them.  The passage has 19 keycaps on a screen, so the 17th
to 19th (Ctrl-s, Ctrl-S, F12) were empty.  The same list of 16 stood in the
copy of the last frame (last_img of src/term_x11.c) and in the list of the
pictures given to the display in one drawing (eq_done of src/view.c).  The
renderer (src/eqrender.c) also kept at most 128 different pictures: the 129th
request returned no picture and no failure.

Evidence: the owner passage with its comments, put in a document of its own
and opened under Xvfb with an empty cache: before the fix 16 pictures drawn
(the screenshot shows the gaps at Ctrl-s, Ctrl-S and F12), the dump of the
screen has image cells at the gaps, the cache has 17 .png and no .err; after
the fix 19 drawn.  Two keycaps whose legends differ only in case have two
different cache names (the name is a hash of display, size, colors and the
exact text) and two different pictures.

3. The fix of the cause
-----------------------
The three lists grow as needed.  The table of the renderer holds up to 4096
different pictures, each in a block of its own so that the address of one
does not change when the table grows (the pictures that a frame holds point
into it).

4. The fallback
---------------
A job of the renderer can fail: LaTeX says an error, LaTeX is not installed,
the job takes more than 5 seconds (every job has this limit, not only the
LaTeX marker; it was 20 seconds), or the picture is refused.  The marker of an
Equation (9), a Picture (25), a Keycap (37) or a LaTeX marker (41) whose
display mode is Inline and whose picture failed is then drawn in its Short
form, the rule of the owner of 21 November 2026 (marker_failed_fn of marker.h,
used by marker_render): [Key: Ctrl-s], [PIC name], [EQN text], [LaTeX
instructions].  It is never an empty box.  A marker that the rule table of
LATEXMARKER-PICTURES.txt never draws as a picture is the Short form at once,
with no job and no entry in the Error Log.  A Picture whose file cannot be
used keeps the error label of picmissing; the other display modes (Diamond,
Name, Short, Long, Hidden) ask for no picture and draw the marker by their
mode as before.

While the job runs, the line holds the cells that are reserved for the
picture (a short time).  When the job ends, success or not, the Editor lays
the text out again (the picture or the label may have another width): the
label never overlaps its neighbours.

The Error Log of the run gets one warning for each failed marker: "The Keycap
in block B, line L cannot be drawn as a picture, so it is drawn as Short:
FIRST LINE OF THE MESSAGE" (the same wording as the LaTeX marker of
LATEXMARKER-PICTURES.txt, which reuses the same renderer).  When the cursor
is on the marker the message line says "Keycap drawn as Short: ..." (or
Picture, Equation).

5. Tests
--------
tests/xfallback.py (xtest): 19 keycaps of the owner passage and 6 more (25 in
all) on a screen are all pictures, with ink and no white pixel; Ctrl-s and
Ctrl-S are two pictures; with a fake pdflatex and latex that exit 1 the three
markers are drawn in the Short form, with ink, one log entry each and the
message line; in the modes Diamond, Name, Short, Long and Hidden the marker is
drawn by its mode and nothing is logged; a job of 30 seconds fails after 5.
tests/keycapfall.py (smoke): the terminal Editor and the files of this
document.  Every check was shown to fail with its feature turned off (the
mutations are in the report of task 48).
