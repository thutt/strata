THE EDITORIAL AUDIT OF THE REFERENCE MANUAL

Date of the audit: 21 November 2026.  The tool is manual/checkeditorial.py
(rules E1 to E18, run by build.sh check-style); the test is
tests/editorial.py; the terms are in docs/MANUAL-TERMS.txt.  The numbers
below were made with the final rules on the sources of the day before the
pass and on the sources after it.

THE ATTRIBUTES OF A GREAT REFERENCE MANUAL

  The ten of the request, and the ones that were added (marked +).

  1. Accurate and complete: every key, panel, field, setting and message
     once, with default, range and effect; nothing that the program does
     not do.  (The statement of at most eight pictures to a line was
     removed: the Editor has no such limit.)
  2. One voice of terms and one spelling (docs/MANUAL-TERMS.txt, E8).
  3. Typography: keycaps, program font, units, spaces, dashes, quotation
     marks, heading case, list and table punctuation (E6, E7, E10, E11,
     E15).
  4. Technical writing style: first sentence states the thing, present
     tense, no filler, no narration, no hedges, no contractions (E1 to E5,
     E12, E14).
  5. Predictable structure: entries of one kind follow one pattern
     (reviewed by sample; not enforced by a tool).
  6. Findable: contents, index, glossary, cross references by the
     generator (E9, E18); no typed chapter or appendix number.
  7. Examples that are correct (reviewed by sample).
  8. No dead or duplicate text (E3, E13).
  9. Verifiable: every rule is a check with a test that fails when the
     rule is broken (tests/editorial.py).
 10. Reader's aids: "How this manual is written" (section 1.1) now tells
     the notation of the program font, messages, measures, bold names and
     the section sign.
  + 11. Tables that print as written: a row is not cut in the middle of a
     cell, a command does not go over the lines of a cell, a column is as
     wide as its text (E15, E16, E17 and the widths of 49 tables).
  + 12. No nested parentheses in a cross reference: a reference inside
     parentheses is the sign and the number only (E18, {n:label}).
  + 13. The same checks reach every chapter, the Messages chapter too (it
     was skipped by the style check).
  + 14. A document that is stable under edition: the developer edition and
     the release edition both pass check-edition after every change.

DEFECTS OF THE PRINTED MANUAL THAT THE TOOL FOUND

  - 25 table rows were cut in the middle of a cell: a line that began in the
    first column was the rest of the cell above it, and the table printed
    it as a row of its own (for example the check of the settings
    directory in appendix B and the Retrieve table of chapter 2).
  - Seven cells of the Messages chapter printed a bar: "No space | left on
    device", because a command in the program font went over the lines of a
    table and the bar of the next line became text.
  - Cross references inside parentheses printed a title in parentheses
    inside the parentheses, 755 times.
  - The Messages chapter, a tenth of the manual, was not checked by the
    style rules at all.

THE NOTATION

  One notation of a menu path: the keycap, then the entries, separated by
  commas ({k:F12}, File, Save).  A message of a program is in double quotes
  as shown; an italic word in it is text that varies.  The program font is
  for file names, directories, commands of the system, settings and the
  values of a setting.  A measure is a number, a space and the unit.  A
  reference to a section is the sign and the number, with the title after
  it in parentheses; inside parentheses, the sign and the number.

OPEN POINTS

  - The statements of the manual about the Printer field, Ins in a pop-up
    list, envelopes and templates were not compared with the program one by
    one: this pass changed the form of the text, not its facts, except the
    ones named above.
  - Where a table has rows that are one logical row (the keys of chapter 1),
    the tool cannot tell a continuation from a new row; the 25 defects that
    were found had a first cell that began in lower case.
  - The index has no See and See also entries for the two names of a thing;
    that needs a decision of the author.
