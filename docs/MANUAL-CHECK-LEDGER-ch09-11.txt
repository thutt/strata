LEDGER OF THE READING OF CHAPTERS 9, 10 AND 11 (EQUATIONS, CONVERSION,
MESSAGES) OF THE REFERENCE MANUAL

This ledger follows docs/MANUAL-CHECK-LEDGER-README.txt, which says what
a ledger line is and what the notation of the state means.

Date: 21 November 2026.  Every section of the three chapters was read in
the source, and each statement of fact was compared
with the program (the source of src, or the program run from the debug
build).  Sections: 138.  Sections read: 138.  Sections changed: 38.

How the facts were checked, for the whole of a chapter:
  Chapter 9: the limits and refusals against eqrender.c, latex.c and png.h;
  the 22 commands of the screen list and the 13 environments against the
  lists of the program; every equation of the manual (571) against the list
  of refused commands and environments.
  Chapter 10: the messages against filemenu.c, readfile.c, msdoc.c and
  main.c; the command line text mode by running stred --text.
  Chapter 11: every message of every table against the strings of src (849
  rows; the rest of the false hits are placeholders); the exit statuses and
  the usage lines by running stred, strprint and dviview; the order of the
  rows of every table (12 tables were not sorted and are now).

Chapter 9, the tables of the commands (543 entries in 14 tables and the 14
worked examples): one test document for the Source column and one for the
Result column of every table was made by script, one equation to a
paragraph, and printed with strprint (no printer): all 30 documents print
with no error and no warning, and for every table and for the examples the
pages of the two documents are equal pixel for pixel, so that the Result
column is what the Source column prints.  The Source and the Result text of
every row are also equal as text (after the display style that some tables
put in front).

The pages of the final build that were read at 110 dpi (single edition):
the limits and the tables of the printed checks (9.21, 9.22), delimiters
and accents (9.15, 9.16), matrices (9.19), the messages of the Editor (11.2),
the reasons of refused values (11.5.3), the Document Manager footer and
boxes (11.9), the Setup refusals (11.9.3), the check screen (11.10.2), the
Conversion guide and messages (10.1, 10.10).  All the tables of the
