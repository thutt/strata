EDIT, STRUCTURED AND EDIT, UNSTRUCTURED: WHAT THE PROGRAM DOES
==============================================================

The owner asked (W62): "The text says so, but does it actually do that?
Test it."  The Reference Manual (manual/src/edit.src, section "Structured and
Unstructured Edit") says that Edit, Structured turns an unstructured
document into one section, and that Edit, Unstructured turns a structured one
back only while it has one section and no subsections.  The test is
tests/structconv.py (part of the smoke runner).

THE ANSWER
----------

Yes, with one exception in the text, which was wrong and is corrected.

  Edit, Structured (F12, Edit, Structured; there is no key):
    - The text becomes one section: the body records are S + S, the blocks
      of the text, and -.  The first block is the headline of the section.
    - The status line shows STRUCTURED EDIT and Sect: 1, the headline is
      numbered 1, the cursor is at the start of the document, the document
      is marked as changed, and the saved *B record says 1.
    - No text is lost: the records of the blocks, with their markers, tables
      (CW, CB, CL, CR records) and Picture markers, stand unchanged between
      the new S and the -.
    - The title page records (=1, S, the title) stay before the new
      section.

  Edit, Unstructured (F12, Edit, Unstructured):
    - It is offered only when the document has one section with no
      subsections.  For a document of two sections, or of one section with a
      subsection, the entry is not in the Edit menu, and the letter U in the
      menu does nothing: the document is not changed and there is no
      message.
    - The section and its group are taken away: the body is S and the
      blocks.  The headline stays as the first block without a number, the
      status line shows UNSTRUCTURED EDIT, the cursor is at the start, the
      document is marked as changed, the saved *B record says 0, and the
      title page and the text are unchanged.

  Undo takes either command back in one step.  A round trip (Structured and
  then Unstructured) gives back the body of the first document, byte for
  byte.

WHAT WAS WRONG IN THE TEXT
--------------------------

The table of messages of the Editor (manual/src/messages1.src) listed "A
document with several sections cannot be made unstructured", as the
refusal of Structured or Unstructured on a document of more than one
section.  The program never shows it: the Edit menu has no Unstructured
entry for such a document, so ed_set_structured() is not called with 0 for
it.  The message is still in src/editor.c as a guard that cannot be reached
from the menus (the function has no other caller).  Decision: the program
is right (the entry is simply not offered, which the text of the section
already said) and the text was wrong.  The row of the message table was
taken out, the index entry was reworded, and the caution of the section now
says that until the document is one section the entry is not in the menu,
the letter U does nothing and there is no message.

THE MUTATIONS
-------------

Each was made in src/editor.c, built, the test run, and the change undone:

  M1  Structured does nothing (return 0 at the start of
      ed_set_structured for structured != 0):           11 checks fail.
  M2  doc_can_unstructure() always 1 (Unstructured
      offered for every structured document):            6 checks fail.
  M3  doc_can_unstructure() always 0 (never offered):   10 checks fail.
  M4  the cursor goes to the last block instead of the
      start:                                              7 checks fail.
  M4b the cursor is not reset:                            7 checks fail.
  M5  the *B record of the header is not rewritten:       4 checks fail.
  M7  the closing - is not taken away by Unstructured:    4 checks fail.
  M8  the S record is kept by Unstructured:               4 checks fail.

One mutation does not change the result: taking doc_touch() out of
ed_set_structured() leaves the check "the document is marked as changed"
passing, because doc_insert_item() and doc_delete_items() already mark the
document.  The check still fails when the feature is off (M1, M3).
