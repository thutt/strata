KEYS OF STRATA: EVERY KEY THAT DOES SOMETHING
=============================================

This list names every key that does something in Strata.  It is the source of
the key leaflet.  A row can name several keys that do the same.

Columns
  KEY   The key as the leaflet writes it.  A semicolon separates keys that do
        the same.  Keypad is always "Keypad".
  AT    Where the key works:
          ed  the Editor (structured or unstructured text)
          hl  the Editor while an area is highlighted (F3, F4, F7, Alt-F4,
              Ctrl-X, Ctrl-F3, Ctrl-F4, Autosort)
          eq  the note, comment and equation editor (the same editor, with
              Ins to keep and Esc to restore)
          mn  the F12 menu bar          gr  the Marker Commands grid
          pn  a panel (form) of the Editor   ls  a list, a one-line question
          hp  the help viewer           pg  the pager (text pages)
          dm  the Document Manager (and the Editor when it has no file)
          vw  the Viewer of Preview
          sp, th  Spell, Thesaurus      all  everywhere
  VER   both = the terminal and the X11 version; term = terminal only;
        x11 = X11 only.
  WHAT  What the key does.

How the two versions name the keys
  - The X11 version sees every modifier.  Keypad +, Keypad - and Keypad *
    arrive as Alt-+, Alt-- and Alt-* (the outline keys).  Esc loses its
    modifiers; Backspace keeps only Alt; Tab with Shift is back-tab; Enter
    and Keypad Enter are the same.
  - The terminal version takes the cursor keys, Home, End, Ins, Del, PgUp,
    PgDn and F1 to F12 with Shift, Alt and Ctrl, Alt-key as Esc and the key,
    and Ctrl-letter as the control key.  Terminals and window managers often
    keep Alt-F keys, Shift-Ins, F11 or F12 for themselves: Strata offers
    Ctrl-F (a list of the Alt-F commands), Ctrl-F12 (Marker), Alt-O
    (Overtype), Alt-I (Italics) and Alt-< Alt-> (move a section).

THE MODIFIER RULES (terminal version)
-------------------------------------
  Cursor keys.  Left and Right: Ctrl wins over Shift, Alt is ignored.  Up,
  Down, PgUp, PgDn: Ctrl and Alt are ignored; Shift has a meaning (section
  moves).  Home and End: Shift wins over Ctrl, Alt is ignored.
  Function keys.  Alt (alone, with Shift or with Ctrl) is the Alt-F table.
  Ctrl-F1 (Learn), Ctrl-F3, F4, F5 (library), Ctrl-F10 (Undo), Ctrl-F11
  (Redo) and F12 are special.  F10 and F11 have no command without Ctrl.
  Ins, Del.  Shift, Ctrl and Alt are ignored: Ins appends a section (Shift-
  Ins a subsection), Del deletes.
  Letters.  Ctrl-letter is the control key (Ctrl-I is Tab, Ctrl-M and
  Ctrl-J are Enter, Ctrl-H is the hanging indent).  Alt-letter is a macro
  key, except Alt-I (Italics) and Alt-O (Overtype).

MENU PATHS (letters: the first letter of an entry chooses it)
-------------------------------------------------------------
  F12 File: Save, Retrieve, New, Delete, Import, Export.
  F12 Global: Format, Page, Typeface, Marker, Options, Named (Edit, Delete).
  F12 Edit: Title, Structured or Unstructured, Section or Document.
  F12 Print: Draft, Final, Preview (Document, Block, Section).
  F12 Tools: Macros (Edit, Delete, Learn, Save, Retrieve), Library (Edit,
  Delete, Save, Retrieve), Spell, Thesaurus, Autosort
  (Section, Table), Count.
  F12 Quit.
