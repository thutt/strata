# Names that the packages define and that are not on the sheet, each with
# the reason.  The check (checkmathsyms.py) fails for a name that is in
# neither this file nor the list.
#
# Names of fonts from before the present ones: the sheet has what replaces
# them.
\Bbb | the old name of \mathbb
\bold | the old name of \mathbf
\frak | the old name of \mathfrak
# Pieces that the packages use to build other symbols: they do nothing alone.
\braceld | a piece of a large brace
\bracelu | a piece of a large brace
\bracerd | a piece of a large brace
\braceru | a piece of a large brace
\joinrel | joins two symbols (the long arrows are made with it)
\mapstochar | the bar of \mapsto
\math | an inner command of the packages
\primfrac | an inner command of the packages
\qopname | an inner command of the packages: it needs its arguments
\tmspace | an inner command of the packages: it needs its arguments
\varlim | an inner command of the packages: not defined for a user
\MultiIntegral | an inner command of the packages: it needs its arguments
# Commands for the text of a page, not for a formula.
\downbracefill | fills a line in text, not in an equation
\upbracefill | fills a line in text, not in an equation
\leftarrowfill | fills a line in text, not in an equation
\rightarrowfill | fills a line in text, not in an equation
# Commands that are made for a displayed equation with numbers.
\allowdisplaybreaks | for a page of displayed equations
\displaybreak | for a page of displayed equations
\numberwithin | numbers the equations of a document by section
\eqref | refers to a numbered equation
\shoveleft | moves a line of a displayed equation
\shoveright | moves a line of a displayed equation
