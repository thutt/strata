AVERY LABEL SHEETS: THE DATA BEHIND THE LABEL TEMPLATES
=======================================================

Written 2026-11-21 for the owner's request of 2026-11-20 (worklist item W69):
make a template for common Avery sticker label sheets; research the sheets,
the size of the stickers, the number of stickers across and down.  The data
are in tests/labeldata.py, the templates are made from them by
tests/mklabels.py (the committed copies are in templates/labels/avery/us and
templates/labels/avery/a4, docs/TEMPLATES.txt), the table of this
document is made from the data by tests/mklabelsdoc.py, and tests/labels.py
compares the data, this document, the templates and the manual.

How to read this document: every number says where it comes from, with the
letter of its source (section 2).  A number that no published source gave is
not used; the sheets for which none was found are listed at the end.  The
web was read on 2026-11-21 with the search tool and the page reader of the
session.


1.  WHAT WAS FOUND, AND WHAT WAS NOT
------------------------------------

  - Avery's own pages give the size of the sticker and the number on a
    sheet and nothing else.  The product page of the 5160
    (https://www.avery.com/products/labels/5160) lists "1" x 2-5/8"",
    "Rectangle" and "30 per Sheet", and no margin and no pitch.  The help
    article on the margins of the Word templates
    (https://www.avery.com/help/article/margin-settings-word-template) says
    that the margins, the pitch and the size are in the Details of the label
    options of Word, and in the product specifications of a blank template:
    they are not printed on the page.  The blank templates themselves are
    files for Word, Illustrator, InDesign, Photoshop, Pages and PDF, which
    were not read (the page reader gives text only).
  - The pages of Avery in the United Kingdom and of Avery Zweckform refuse
    the page reader (HTTP 403), and the Zweckform catalogue (a PDF of
    several hundred pages) was not read.  The product title of the L7160 on
    avery.co.uk says 63.5 x 38.1 mm.
  - Avery's pages name no list of the best selling sheets (the site's own
    index, https://www.avery.com/llms.txt, says "None are listed").  The
    criterion of this document is therefore the request itself: the kinds
    that it names (address, shipping, return address, name badge, file
    folder, round, CD or DVD, mini) and the sheets of the two sources below
    that are of those kinds, and that a template can show (business cards,
    cards, tent cards and index tabs are not stickers on a sheet of
    paper).  The 5160 family and the L7160 family are the ones that the
    request names first.
  - The geometry of every sheet therefore comes from one published data set,
    the label templates of gLabels (an open source label program) that give
    for every sheet the size, the number across and down, the margins and the
    pitch.  It was made by measuring and by Avery's data, and it differs
    from the older data of Microsoft in a few places (section 3).


2.  THE SOURCES  (all read on 2026-11-21)
------------------------------------------

  U   the US Letter sheets of gLabels (template file avery-us-templates.xml)
      https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jimevins/glabels-qt/master/templates/
      avery-us-templates.xml
      The sizes are in inches; some in points.  Lengths: label size, x0 and
      y0 (the left and the top margin: the position of the first sticker),
      dx and dy (the pitch: from one sticker to the next one across and
      down).  A sheet that the file gives as an alias of another one ("equiv")
      has the layout of that one; the numbers of the aliases are listed under
      the sheet.
  I   the A4 sheets of gLabels (avery-iso-templates.xml)
      https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jimevins/glabels-qt/master/templates/
      avery-iso-templates.xml
      The file gives no unit for a number that has none: they are points
      (1/72 inch), as the paper of the file is 595.28 x 841.89.
  K   Microsoft Knowledge Base article Q146892, Avery LaserJet and InkJet
      Product Information, reviewed 1997-07-31 (the numbers of Word for
      Windows 95)
      https://techshelps.github.io/MSDN/KB/Source/word/q146892.htm
      It gives the size, the number across and down and the pitch of 80
      sheets, no margin.  It is used to check the pitch of the sheets that
      it has (the line "check" below), and nothing is taken from it.
  A   Avery's own pages: the product page of the 5160 (see above), the
      product titles at avery.co.uk for the L7160 (63.5 x 38.1 mm).  They
      confirm the size and the count; no number of the table is from them.

The paper sizes are US Letter, 8.5 x 11 in (the sheets of U), and A4,
210 x 297 mm (the sheets of I); a document records them in thousandths of an
inch: 8500 x 11000 and 8268 x 11693.


3.  CONFLICTS BETWEEN THE SOURCES
---------------------------------

  K and U differ for three sheets by more than half a millimetre: the 5160
  (K has 2.75 in across, U has 2.78125 in; the left margin of U is 0.15625
  in), the 5197 (4.188 in against 4.125 in) and the 5295 (3.937 in against
  3.9063 in across and 3.333 in against 3.3594 in down).  The templates use
  U for every sheet: it is one set of numbers that has the margins that K
  does not have, and a pitch of K with a margin of U would be a mixture.  The
  reading of a sheet of U is the sheet that the data set's author measured;
  the owner should compare one printed sheet with a template when a printer
  is at hand (no sheet was printed here: the tests measure PDFs).
  The sticker of the L7160 is 181.4 x 108.0 points (64.0 x 38.1 mm) in I and
  63.5 x 38.1 mm on the product title of Avery: 0.5 mm wider in I.  The
  template uses I, so that the first sticker and its pitch are the same data.


4.  THE LAYOUT OF A TEMPLATE
----------------------------

  See tests/mklabels.py, and the manual.  The sticker is the position that the
  data give; the text starts 2 mm from its left edge and 1.5 mm from its top
  edge (1 mm and 0.5 mm on a small sticker, and the corner of the square that
  fits in the circle on a round one).  A template is blank: it has one block
  for each sticker, and no text.

6.  THE LAYOUT: WHAT WAS MEASURED, AND WHAT WAS DECIDED
-------------------------------------------------------

  Three ways to put one block on each sticker were considered.

  a) A table with fixed cell sizes.  The width of a column of a table is a
     number of ruler cells, and a cell is a part of the width of the text
     (an eightieth by default, a tenth, twelfth or fifteenth of an inch at
     the other settings of the ruler).  Measured on a PDF of a table of ten
     rows and three columns on US Letter paper (the text 8.1875 in wide, one
     cell 2.6 mm): the first column starts 6.07 mm from the left edge, and
     the second column starts 69.19, 71.81, 74.40 or 77.00 mm further for
     columns of 25, 26, 27 and 28 cells.  The pitch of the 5160 across is
     70.64 mm: the best columns are 1.45 mm too narrow or 1.17 mm too wide,
     so no number of cells puts a column within half a millimetre.  A row of
     the table is as high as its lines (4.22 mm for the lines of one line at
     10 points here), not as high as a sticker.  Not used.
  b) Columns with column breaks.  A block can have columns, but their width
     is again a number of cells, and a column break moves the text to the
     next column on the same page, not to the next row.  Not used.
  c) Winding columns (a Strata mechanism: the block property that sets the
     number of columns a block is set in, with the gutter between them in
     thousandths of an inch).  The columns have equal width and the gutter
     is exact, so the width of the text is the number of stickers across
     times the width of the text of a sticker, plus the gutters.  Used.
     Down a column the pitch is made by the lines of a block and the space
     after the block (hundredths of a line, the line pitch measured in the
     PDF for each text size: tests/labeldata.py).  The sum of the spaces is
     rounded, not each one, so that the rows do not drift.

  Measured (tests/labels.py): every one of the 48 sheets was printed twice to
  a PDF, once with a text in every line of every sticker and once with a
  probe in the first line of the first, the middle and the last sticker only.
  The distance of the left and top of every probe from the place that the data
  give (the sticker, the distance of the text from its edge, and the top of
  the glyph box of the text size) was at most 0.036 mm, the sheet was one page
  in every case, and every text was inside its sticker.  The test asks for
  0.5 mm (the request) and for 0.1 mm (so that a drift shows).

  The sheet in the template is filled down the first column, then down the
  next: that is the order of the blocks.  Avery's own order (across the rows)
  is not possible with winding columns.

  Text that is too long.  A block holds the lines that fit in the sticker
  (the column Lines of the manual).  The Printer cannot cut a block at the
  height of a sticker, so a text of more lines pushes the stickers below it
  down, and the stickers of that column after it are off their place: the
  text is not cut, and the fault can be seen in the Preview.  The manual says it.  A line that
  is too wide wraps and has the same effect.

  The page number is not printed: the template has a header that is one
  space, because the Printer prints the page number of a document that has no
  header and no footer text, and on a sheet that has no margin (5165) the
  number would be on the sticker.


7.  DECISIONS AND OPEN ITEMS
----------------------------

  - One template for each product number that the source shows as the same
    sheet (101 templates for 48 sheets); the name is the paper and the lower
    case number: label-us-5160, label-a4-l7160.  The description starts with
    "Avery", the number, the kind, the count and the paper.
  - The list of New finds a sheet by typing: the first entry that has a word
    that begins with the typed text, in its name or its description.
  - Round stickers and discs: the box that holds them is the position; the
    text starts in the corner of the square inside the circle.  The hole of
    a disc is not left free.  The sheet 5931 has spine labels too, which have
    no template (the disc is the template).
  - The ten stickers across of the L4730 are more than a block winds in (nine
    columns at most): no template.
  - The sheets 5390, 5392, L7159, L7106, L7418 and 6240 of the request were
    not found in a source that has numbers; no template.
  - Which of K and U is right for the 5160, the 5197 and the 5295 can only
    be decided with a printed sheet.
