LATEX MARKERS DRAWN AS PICTURES (DESIGN)
========================================

Status: implemented in the X11 version of the Editor.  The terminal
version is not changed: it draws no pictures.

CHANGE OF 21 NOVEMBER 2026 (the rule of the owner, which replaces the
setting of the first design where the two disagree): the LaTeX marker has
the display mode Inline, like the Equation, the Picture and the Keycap, and
Inline is the DEFAULT mode of the type.  The X11 Editor draws a LaTeX marker
as a picture only when its mode is Inline; in every other mode (Short, Name,
Long, Diamond, Hidden) it is drawn by that mode and no job is started.  When
the picture is not made (LaTeX not installed, an error, 5 seconds, no ink,
too large) the marker is drawn in its SHORT form, and the message line says
"LaTeX marker drawn as Short: REASON".  The same rule holds for the
Equation, the Picture and the Keycap (KEYCAP-FALLBACK.txt).  The setting
"Draw LaTeX Markers As Pictures?" and its record OLP do not exist any more:
a document with OLP is read, the record is ignored and it is not written
again.  The Printer setting latex_markers=no (which forbids the printing of
LaTeX markers) is kept: the Editor draws no picture for a marker that
setting does not allow, and says so.  The terminal Editor draws a marker
whose mode is Inline in its Short form.

SECOND CHANGE OF 21 NOVEMBER 2026 (the rule of the owner, which replaces the
table of commands that were never drawn): "Can not you send it to LaTeX and
let it error out?  If it produces text, treat it as a graphic and render
it."  The table is gone.  There is one rule (section 1), and the Editor
does not look at what the text says.

1. The one rule
---------------
The text of a LaTeX marker (type 41, MARKERS.txt) is given to LaTeX alone,
typeset in a box.  Then:

  - when LaTeX reports an error, the marker is drawn in its Short form, the
    first line of the error is the reason ("LaTeX says: Undefined control
    sequence.") and a warning with the place of the marker (block and line)
    is written to the Error Log;
  - when the job takes more than 5 seconds, the marker is drawn in its Short
    form ("it took more than 5 seconds");
  - when the result has no ink, the marker is drawn in its Short form ("it
    makes nothing visible"): a marker that only sets a state, makes a
    definition, breaks a page, or makes only space gives nothing to see;
  - otherwise the result is a picture and it is drawn.

What the text says is not looked at.  A page break, vertical space, a
section command or a definition either makes LaTeX report an error or
leaves nothing visible in a box of its own, so the marker is drawn in its
Short form without any list of commands that are refused beforehand.  A
marker that has ink and also has vertical space, such as
\vspace{3ex}\stratalogo[3]\vspace{3ex}, is drawn as a picture of the part
that has ink.  The marker is for a piece of text of a line; the space
between blocks belongs in a Whitespace marker, and the generators of the
manual and of the story sample put the space of the title page in
Whitespace markers (the text of the LaTeX marker is only
\stratalogo[3]).

The height of the picture is not limited.  A picture that is taller than the
lines around it is a block and is drawn like a Picture marker that is tall:
on rows of its own (section 5).  The author sees what the print will be.

2. The decision
---------------
One step before the job: the comments of the text (from a percent sign to
the end of the line) are taken out and the lines are joined with one space
(src/latexmark.c); a text that is longer than 900 bytes after that is not
sent ("its text is too long to draw as a picture"), and an empty text is
drawn in the Short form ("it is empty").  Everything else is the job
(eqrender.c, display 4): pdflatex on the text alone, with the preamble of
the Printer, in a box; the box is shipped as a page of exactly its size
plus one point of border; pdftoppm makes the PNG.  The reasons that the
Editor keeps are the ones in section 7.

The safety of the job is not a rule about the meaning of the text and
stays: pdflatex runs with -no-shell-escape, openin_any=p and openout_any=p,
by its full path, in a private temporary directory (mode 0700), in a process
group of its own that is stopped when it takes more than 5 seconds.

The Printer has its own rule for the markers: a document or a setting can
forbid them (the setting latex_markers=no, the option --no-latex-markers):
then no text of a marker is given to LaTeX.  The Editor follows it: with
that setting every LaTeX marker is drawn in the Short form.

3. What the Editor draws, a table
---------------------------------
Each text is the text of one LaTeX marker, alone in a line "NN [marker]
end".  P means a picture, S means drawn in the Short form.  The test
tests/xlmpic.py makes this table from a document, under Xvfb, and checks
that what the Editor draws is what the table says.  The S lines at the end
are what LaTeX does with texts that a table used to refuse beforehand: an
error, or nothing visible.

4. The tallest and the widest picture
-------------------------------------
There is no height limit of the rule.  The picture job makes at most 4000
by 4000 pixels, as the job of a Picture marker does (pdftoppm -W and -H);
a picture that would be larger is refused with "it is larger than 4000 by
4000 pixels".  The layout reserves at most 300 rows and 300 columns of cells
for a picture (EQ_MAX_CELLS in ui.c), as for every picture.  The time of the
job is limited to 5 seconds.

5. The picture
--------------
Made like a keycap or an equation: a job of eqrender.c (display 4), in the
background, one job at a time, polled from the event loop of the window.

  - Document: the head is the preamble of the Printer at the text size of
    the document (latex_snippet_head in latex.c: the same packages, the
    symbols, the macros of the Printer, the Strata name and logo, without
    the page geometry, the headers and the other things that belong to the
    pages).  Math mode is not assumed: the text is set as it stands.
  - The text is set in a box of its own (\hbox) in the color of the text of
    the window, and its width, height and depth are written to the log of
    LaTeX.  The box is shipped as a page of exactly its size plus one
    point of border, on the color of the window, and pdftoppm makes the
    PNG, as for the keycaps.  The depth of the box below the baseline is
    kept with the picture, so that the picture stands on the baseline of
    the text.
  - Size: the text size of the document (the record PS, 11 points by
    default) is the size of one line of the window, so the picture has the
    size that the same text has in the printed line relative to the other
    text.  The picture is made at the resolution of the window text,
    scaled by 10 over the text size.
  - Place: a picture that needs at most four rows in its line (the rows
    above and below the baseline included) is a word of the line: baseline
    aligned, and the line grows only as far as the picture needs.  A picture
    that needs more rows is a block (eqn_is_display in layout.c: the same
    layout as a displayed equation or a tall Picture marker): the text
    before the marker ends its line, the picture takes rows of its own,
    centred, and the text after the marker goes on in the line below it.  A
    picture is never more than 300 cells wide or high (EQ_MAX_CELLS in
    ui.c); one that does not fit in what is left of the line starts the
    next line.
  - Cache: in memory and in the cache directory of the equations
    (~/.cache/strata/eqn), by a key made of the display, the resolution,
    the colors, the text size and the text of the marker.  Changing the
    text or the text size makes a new key; the same text in two markers is
    made once; a document that is opened again shows the pictures at once.
    The key of a LaTeX marker has the number of the rule (r2 since the one
    rule of section 1): a refusal that an earlier rule kept in the cache,
    such as a block taller than two lines, must not hold a marker in the
    Short form after the rule has changed.
  - Never waits for a key: the job runs in a child process; until the
    picture is ready the marker is drawn by its settings, and the window is
    drawn again when it is.  Only markers that are on the screen are asked
    for.
  - Limits: a job that takes more than 5 seconds is stopped with all its
    child processes and its temporary directory is removed; the marker
    stays drawn by settings and the reason ("latex took more than 5
    seconds") is kept.  The files are made in a private temporary
    directory (mode 0700) that the Editor makes before the job.
  - Safety: pdflatex runs with -no-shell-escape, openin_any=p and
    openout_any=p, by its full path (src/sysprog.c), in a private
    temporary directory and a process group of its own.  A command that
    would read or write a file outside the directory, or run a program, is
    stopped by those settings (LaTeX reports the error and the marker is
    drawn in the Short form); the text is not looked at beforehand.
  - The cursor: the picture is one object, like an equation or a picture
    (PICTURE-CURSOR.txt): one key onto it and one key off it, and a box
    surrounds it when the cursor is on it.  The picture of a block is one
    stop of the cursor for all its rows.

6. The display mode
-------------------
Global Format, Marker Display Modes panel (page 2 of 3): the mode of the type
LaTeX is Diamond, Name, Short, Long, Hidden or Inline, Inline by default.  A
mode other than Inline is kept in the document as the record OM41,N (the
default is not written).  The old setting "Draw LaTeX Markers As Pictures?"
(record OLP, written only when it was No) is read and dropped.  The terminal
Editor ignores the mode Inline and draws the Short form.  A marker whose mode
is Hidden stays hidden: its picture is not asked for.  Changing the text of
the marker makes a new key, so the picture is refreshed.

7. When it cannot be drawn
--------------------------
In each of these cases the marker is drawn by its display mode, with no
dialog and no delay (in the Short form; with the mode Short, Name, Long,
Diamond or Hidden no picture is tried at all):

  - LaTeX (pdflatex and pdftoppm) is not installed (the test removes the
    program folders with STRATA_PROGRAM_DIRS, tests/progpath.py);
  - the Printer setting forbids LaTeX markers;
  - the text is empty, or too long (900 bytes after the comments are taken
    out);
  - LaTeX reports an error: the first line of the message of LaTeX is
    written to the log of the run as a warning with the place of the marker
    (block and line), and it is the message line when the cursor is on the
    marker ("LaTeX marker drawn as Short: LaTeX says: Undefined control
    sequence.");
  - the job takes more than 5 seconds ("it took more than 5 seconds");
  - the result has no ink, or the box has no width ("it makes nothing
    visible");
  - the picture is larger than 4000 by 4000 pixels.

The reasons that are not an error of LaTeX are written to the log as debug
messages, once for each text.  They are in the message line when the cursor
is on the marker.

8. Tests
--------
  - tests/lmtest.c: the preparation of the text (comments, lines, the
    length).  Nothing in the text is refused beforehand, so there is no
    table to check.
  - tests/xlmpic.py (Xvfb): the table of section 3 with what the Editor
    draws for each text (ink: a picture; an error or no ink: the Short
    form), including the markers that a table used to refuse; a marker with
    the TeX logo is a picture whose pixels differ from the label and equal
    the picture of the cache, which matches a reference render of the text;
    an error draws the Short form and is in the log with its place; a page
    break is given to LaTeX, which reports an error; LaTeX missing draws the
    Short form; the cache is used on the second draw (the compiles are
    counted by a stand-in of pdflatex); a slow stand-in (sleeps 20 seconds)
    never holds a key for more than 0.3 seconds and is stopped at 5 seconds;
    the modes Diamond, Name, Long and Hidden start no job; a picture that is
    taller than four rows is a block on rows of its own, the lines around it
    are not overlapped, it has ink, and the cursor passes it in a few keys;
    the logo of the title page of the story sample is a picture; the
    terminal Editor is unchanged.
  - tests/lmsetting.py: the mode in the panel, the record OM41, the old
    record OLP read and dropped, the terminal Editor.
  - The mutations that were tried against each check are in the report.

9. Decisions
------------
  - (Replaced on 21 November 2026.)  The first design had a Yes or No
    setting in the Marker Format panel.  The owner asked for the rule of
    the Equation, the Picture and the Keycap: the display mode Inline, the
    default, and the Short form when the picture fails.
  - (Replaced on 21 November 2026.)  The first design had a table of the
    commands, environments and switches that are never drawn as pictures.
    The owner asked for one rule: give the text to LaTeX, and draw what has
    ink.  The table, its documentation and the reasons that came from it
    ("it opens a block" and the like) are gone; the reasons are now what
    LaTeX said, "it makes nothing visible", "it took more than 5 seconds"
    and "it is larger than 4000 by 4000 pixels".
  - Hidden stays hidden: a person who set the mode to Hidden does not want
    to see the marker.
  - The picture takes the text size of the document and is scaled to the
    window text, so that a logo is as large on the screen, relative to the
    words around it, as it is on the page.
  - There is no height limit.  A picture that is taller than four rows is a
    block on rows of its own, as a tall Picture marker is: the author sees
    what the print will be.  The limit of the size is that of the picture
    job (4000 by 4000 pixels), the same as that of a Picture marker, and the
    layout limit of 300 cells.
  - Vertical space belongs in a Whitespace marker.  The generators of the
    manual and of the story sample put the space around the logo of the
    title page in Whitespace markers of 7.3 points, and the LaTeX marker
    holds only \stratalogo[3].  (A marker that also holds vertical space is
    still drawn when it has ink.)
  - An error of LaTeX goes to the log as a warning, with the place; any
    other reason goes as a debug message.  Neither opens a dialog.
