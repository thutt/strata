LINE NUMBERING IN LATEX: A SURVEY, AND WHAT STRATA DOES
=======================================================

This is the survey that the worklist item "Line numbering (the LaTeX package
lineno)" asked for before the work.  It states only what was read on this
machine or tried on it.  Where a package is not installed here, it says so
and says nothing else about the package.  Part 1 is the survey; part 2 is
the design that follows from it; part 3 says what was found when lineno met
the LaTeX that Strata writes.

The machine: Debian 13 (trixie), TeX Live 2025, pdfTeX.  The trials of part
1 are in /tmp/lntry (not kept in the repository: they are throw-away
documents; the commands are written down below so that they can be run
again).  The trial document is an article of 8.5 by 11 inches with 1 inch
margins, twelve paragraphs of the lipsum package, one numbered equation
after the sixth paragraph and a two by two tabular after the eighth, so two
pages.  The numbers were read from the PDF with pdftotext -bbox (the words
made only of digits that stand in the left or right margin).


1. THE SURVEY
-------------

1.1 lineno
----------

Name and version: lineno 5.4, dated 2025/01/29, authors Stephan I.
Boettcher, Uwe Lueck and Karl Wette (read from the head of the file).
Where read: the package source, /usr/share/texlive/texmf-dist/tex/latex/
lineno/lineno.sty.  There is no separate PDF manual installed (texdoc has no
entry for it); the documentation is written inside the .sty file as a
documented source, and that is what was read.

What it does: it hooks the output routine of LaTeX and puts a number in the
margin for every line that TeX has set in a paragraph, so the numbers follow
the lines that TeX really breaks, whatever the font, width or hyphenation.
It needs no change of the text, only \linenumbers after \begin{document}.

The options and commands that the source offers (all of them were read; the
ones marked "tried" were run):

  left, right      the side of the number (tried).
  switch, switch*  the number on the outer (inner) margin of a two-sided
                   document (tried, with the class option twoside).
  pagewise         the count starts again at 1 on each page (tried).
  running          one count through the text (tried).
  columnwise       the count starts again in each column of a two-column
                   page (read).
  modulo           only the lines whose number is a multiple of five (tried);
                   \modulolinenumbers[n] sets another step (read).
  modulo*          as modulo, but the first line of a paragraph also (read).
  mathlines        the lines of a displayed equation are numbered (tried).
  sep, width       the distance of the number from the text, and the width
                   of the number box (read; version 5.3 and later).
  \linenumberfont  the font of the number: the default is
                   \normalfont\tiny\sffamily (read, line 1546).
  \linenumbersep   the distance of the number from the text, 10 pt by
                   default (read).
  \thelinenumber   the format of the number, \arabic{linenumber} by default;
                   it can be redefined to \roman, \Roman, \alph or \Alph
                   (read).
  \resetlinenumber  sets the running counter, so that it can start again at
                   a section (read).
  \nolinenumbers   stops the numbering; \linenumbers starts it again (read).
  \linelabel       a label on a line, for references (read; not needed).

Results of the trials (pdfLaTeX, two passes):

  options              page 1       page 2           side
  pagewise             1 to 45      1 to 37          left, x = 58.9 pt
  running              1 to 45      46 to 82         left
  pagewise,modulo      5, 10 ... 45 5, 10 ... 35     left
  pagewise,right       2 to 45      1 to 37          right, x = 556.8 pt
  pagewise,mathlines   42 to 46 on the lines of the equation and after it;
                       the equation takes one number, its own
  switch (twoside)     page 1 right, page 2 left   (odd pages: outer margin)
  switch* (twoside)    page 1 left, page 2 right

What fails or is only partial (from the source, section on arrays and
tabular environments, and from the trials): a standard tabular is one box,
so it takes one number for the whole table (the count steps once) unless the
options edtable or longtable are used; lines inside a box (a minipage, an
\hbox, a \vbox) are not numbered; footnotes are not numbered; the source says
that displayed mathematics works "not too well" without the mathlines
option, and that footnotes and floats needed special care in the output
routine.

1.2 vruler
----------

Name and version: vruler 2.3, October 1996, by Zhuhan Jiang (read from the
head of the file).  Where read: /usr/share/texlive/texmf-dist/tex/latex/
vruler/vruler.sty.  Tried.

What it does: it draws a ruler of numbers at fixed distances at the margin
of every page, whatever the text is: \setvruler[scale][first][step][digits]
[mode] with a distance of 12 pt gave 55 numbers on a page, at x = 22.2 pt.
What it does well: nothing in the text can disturb it (displayed equations,
tables, pictures), and the count is the same on every page.  What fails: the
numbers are not tied to the lines of the text: a line of another height, a
heading or a picture does not move them, so a number is not "the number of
the line beside it".  That is not line numbering as a person who cites a
passage ("line 17") needs it.

1.3 Overlays of the whole page (eso-pic, everypage, tikzpagenodes)
------------------------------------------------------------------

These three packages are installed here (kpsewhich finds eso-pic.sty,
everypage.sty and tikzpagenodes.sty).  They put material on every page at a
fixed place; a ruler of numbers can be made with them, which is the same
method as vruler and has the same limit: the numbers do not follow the
lines.  They were not read further and not tried.

1.4 Pleading-paper packages and classes, numline, ledmac, reledmac
------------------------------------------------------------------

Not installed on this machine (kpsewhich finds no pleading.sty, pleading.cls,
numline.sty, ledmac.sty or reledmac.sty).  They were not read and not tried,
and nothing is claimed about them here.  The idea of court-form pleading
paper (a fixed number of lines on every page, at exact line spacing, with a
ruled margin) is the optional extra of the worklist item: it can be made with
lineno and an exact \baselineskip, see part 2.

1.5 Why lineno
--------------

The owner's decision: use the LaTeX package lineno and support the styles
that it supports.  What the trials add: lineno is the only installed
method that numbers the lines that TeX really sets; it is installed with the
TeX Live of this machine, so the exported file compiles alone; it works with
pdfLaTeX and the DVI route (the Viewer reads the DVI of the same
typesetting); and it has everything the
owner listed: pagewise and running numbers, modulo, left, right, inner and
outer, the font and the distance of the number, and the format.


2. WHAT STRATA DOES
-------------------

The setting lives in the document: the Global Format panels have a third
panel of the global scope, Line Numbering, saved as global F records of the
header (docs/DOC-FORMAT.txt).  A document that has none of the records is
written exactly as before: the LaTeX of every document of tests/data was
compared with the output of the program before the feature, byte for byte.
The letter of the records is Z, because FN is the start of a named format
(a first try with N would have been read as one).

  Field            Record  Values (the first is the default)
  Numbering        FZM     Off, Pagewise, Running, Each Section
  Number Every     FZE     1 (every line); 2 to 99
  Side             FZS     Left, Right, Outer, Inner
  Number Size      FZZ     Tiny, Script, Footnote
  Number Font      FZY     Sans, Roman, Typewriter
  Number Format    FZF     Arabic, Roman (i ii iii), Capital Roman
  Distance         FZD     the distance of the number from the text, in
                           inches and hundredths (0.15 by default)
  Equations        FZQ     No, Yes (the lines of a displayed equation)

The default look is the one the owner asked for: a number in a smaller sans
serif font than the text, so that it is never taken for text (the tiny size
of lineno's own default).  Pagewise numbering, every line, left, are the
choices when Numbering is switched on.  "Each Section" is running numbering
that starts again at 1 at each section of the first level (\resetlinenumber
before each such headline).

A format of letters (\alph) is not offered: LaTeX stops with "Counter too
large" after 26 lines, which would make a Preview fail.  Roman numerals are
unbounded.

Outer and Inner put the number at the outer or the inner margin of a page
that is printed on both sides: odd pages are the right-hand pages.  The
document class is one-sided, so the margins are equal and the numbers
alternate with the parity of the page.

What counts as a line, and what is not numbered (tried on every document of
tests/data and on the sample documents; see part 3 for the trials):

  - Every printed line of a paragraph of text (a block, a headline, a
    wrapped line, a line that ends where the text ends) is a line.
  - A blank line between blocks is not a line: it is vertical space.  An
    empty block takes no number.
  - A Keep block is set in a box, which lineno cannot number.  With
    numbering, the lines of the box are put on the page (\unvbox) instead of
    the box, and then they are numbered; the space is asked for first, so the
    block stays together as before.
  - A Keep block that floats (in a figure) is not numbered.
  - A table (a block of several columns) is a longtable.  The option
    longtable of lineno was tried and breaks the rows (they print on top of
    each other), so tables are not numbered; the count goes on past them.
  - A picture is one line of its paragraph and takes one number.
  - Footnotes and notes, headers, footers, the failed-print banner and the
    Error Log page are not numbered.  The Print Error marks are text of the
    paragraphs; the numbers do not move them.
  - Displayed equations are numbered only with the field Equations (the
    option mathlines): the equation takes one number.


3. WHAT WAS FOUND WITH THE LATEX THAT STRATA WRITES
---------------------------------------------------

The body that the Printer writes is made of paragraphs, so lineno works with
it as it is.  What was tried, with the Printer on the sample documents of
tests/data (all print to the same number of pages with and without numbers):

  - Pagewise numbers need two runs.  The first run gives running numbers; the
    second reads the page of every line from the .aux file.  The Printer
    already runs LaTeX twice when the file has page references; line
    numbering with pagewise numbers (and the outer or inner side, which is
    made of them) asks for that too.
  - The option left, although it is the default, must not be written: given
    with pagewise it makes the numbers running (options are processed in the
    order in which lineno declares them).
  - The Preview, the DVI of the same typesetting: the numbers are the same on
    every page as in the PDF (tests/lineno.py compares them), and the
    Viewer draws them like the text (tools/dviview/tests/run_tests.sh,
    test_lineno: the window agrees with the picture that dvipdfmx makes of
    the DVI, and there is ink at the left margin and none at the right).  A
    screenshot of a Preview of a numbered document in the Viewer under X11
    shows the numbers 1 to 14 in the left margin, small and sans serif, one
    beside each line.
  - A landscape page is numbered like the others.
  - The Error Log page is written with \nolinenumbers.
  - An exported file lists lineno among its packages in the header comment
    and compiles alone to the same numbers (tests/lineno.py).
  - Text left out by the conditional text markers is not in the LaTeX at
    all, so the count follows the text that is printed.

Not numbered exactly: tables (the rows), Keep blocks that float, notes and
the other things in the list of part 2.  What was done about them: tables and
floats have no remedy in lineno (the longtable option breaks the layout);
Keep blocks were made numberable with \unvbox; the count is not disturbed.
