LEDGER OF THE READ-EVERY-SECTION PASS: APPENDICES A TO E, THE GLOSSARY
AND THE KEY REFERENCE

This ledger follows docs/MANUAL-CHECK-LEDGER-README.txt, which says what
a ledger line is and what the notation of the state means.

Date: 21 November 2026.  Part: manual/src/appcmd*.src, appcfg*.src,
appchar.src, appterm.src, appx11.src, glossary.src, cheat.src and
mathcheat.src.  Sections are numbered as in the single document
(A.1 is the first section of Appendix A).

HOW TO READ THIS LEDGER

  Every line is one headline of the part.  The columns are: number, title,
  read, how the facts were checked, what was wrong, what was changed.  Each
  section was read whole, in the source.  The facts were checked against
  the program where the text states one that the program decides: the
  options (the usage text of each program and its parser), the environment
  variables (every getenv), the keys of the settings (config.c), the font
  table (src/fonts.c), the character table (src/layout.c), the menus
  (stred --dump-menus), the timings and limits of the terminal and the X11
  window (term.c, term_x11.c, ui.c, config.c).  A line that says only
  "read" in the how column had no such fact beyond what the manual's own
  sections say; the text was compared with them.  The glossary has 222
  entries; they are one line each and are covered by the glossary lines
  below.
