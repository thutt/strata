MANUAL CHECK LEDGER, CHAPTERS 1 AND 2 (the part ch01-02)

This ledger follows docs/MANUAL-CHECK-LEDGER-README.txt, which says what
a ledger line is and what the notation of the state means.

One line per section of chapter 1 (A View of Strata) and chapter 2 (File).
Columns: source file | label | level | heading | state | how the facts were
checked | what was wrong or changed.

How deep: every section was READ in full, in the source.  The facts of the
sections were checked against the program by the means named on each line:
the compare tables came from scripts (the keys against docs/KEY-MAP.txt, the
menu entries and hints against the output of stred --dump-menus and the MI
tables of src/ui.c, the templates against tests/mktemplates.py) and by grep of
the message texts, field labels and limits in src.  A statement of behavior
that has no message, label or limit to compare (for example how a cursor moves
after a Retrieve, or the order of the steps of Save) was read and not run: the
program was built but those behaviors were not driven by keys in this pass.
That is the limit of this ledger.
Driven in the second round: the revision arithmetic, Autosave, the
recovery of a damaged file, the checklist, Restore Backup, Retrieve &
Insert, Export a LaTeX File and its typesetting, strprint options, the
Viewer under Xvfb, the help viewer.  Driven in the third round: the same
File commands and screens in the X11 Editor under Xvfb (status line,
Ctrl-S, the Save, Retrieve, New, Delete, Import and Export panels, Ctrl-^,
the unit list, the F12 bar, F1 help, the Marker grid, the Go To list and
the key labels that follow Alt); Check Validity set to Yes with a fake
latex that fails (the message and the box) and with the real latex (LaTeX
accepts it); a checklist of a directory of 600 files (FAULT: the 500 are
the first that the file system gives, not the first by name; the text now
says so); strprint --keep, --draft, --double-spaced, --long-list,
--progress and --docdir (--double-spaced doubled the spacing of a letter
and left the plan sample unchanged; --draft ran, its effect needs a
comment marker that no sample has); the Setup panel of the Document
Manager (labels, defaults, the question when Backups Kept is lowered, the
keys backups, autosave, autosave_seconds and workdir of the config file;
FAULT: the field is called Initial Working Directory).
Not driven: the effect of --draft (no sample holds a Comment marker),
and the panels of Setup that chapter 2 does not describe.  Appendix A
agrees with the observed behavior of the options above.
Pages read at 110 dpi: 27, 33, 40, 46, 54, 71, 72, 85, 86, 90, 97, 111, 113.

Sections of the part: 180 (headings of levels 1 to 4 in the ten sources).
