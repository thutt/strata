LEDGER OF THE READ-EVERY-SECTION PASS, CHAPTER 3 (EDIT)

This ledger follows docs/MANUAL-CHECK-LEDGER-README.txt, which says what
a ledger line is and what the notation of the state means.

One entry for each of the 198 sections of chapter 3 (levels 1 to 4,
numbered as printed).  A line that starts with "read; checked" says
what the statements of the section were compared with.  "Not re-run"
means that the text was read and its labels and messages were found
in the program, but that the behavior itself was not run again here.
The rules of the manual (reference style, one name per concept, See,
American spelling) were applied to every section by the lint, which
reports 0 hits.  Key names in running text and in table cells
(not in headlines and picture captions) are keycaps: 11 places
that wrote Alt-F2, Ctrl-F9 and the like in plain letters were
changed, and the entries of the Ctrl-F list are in the program
font.  Table widths of 55 tables follow their content (the page
text is 56 positions wide, so the old widths that added up to 70
left the last column too narrow).
