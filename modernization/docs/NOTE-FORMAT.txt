The Release 2 Note Format panel (probed in the original)
========================================================

Where: F12, Global, Marker opens the Marker Format panel.  Its fields are
Marker Display Modes, Display Hidden Markers?, Note Format, Primary Note Type,
Cross-reference Format, Counter Format, Keycap Format, Screen Capture Format,
Date Format and Time Format.  The ones that say Standard (Note Format,
Counter Format, Keycap Format, Screen Capture Format, Marker Display Modes)
open a panel of their own; Space on the field opens it (Return only moves to
the next field).  Strata's Counter Format and Keycap Format are done
(GLOBAL-MENU.txt).

Primary Note Type: a pick list with Footnotes (the default), Levelnotes and
Endnotes.  It is the kind of note that a new Note marker is.

Note Format: three panels stacked like the Global Format panel (tabs named
Footnote Format, Levelnote Format and Endnote Format; PgUp and PgDn go from
one to the next).  Ins accepts all three, Esc cancels.  The default settings
seen in the original:

Footnote Format
  Pos (the ruler, for tabs in the note), a ruler line
  Attributes: Normal   Justification: Left   Indent: None
  Font: Text (10.0 pitch Generic)
  SPACING   Line: 1.00   Block: 1.50   Note: 1.50
  Reference Tag: \1\    Attributes: Super      (the tag in the text)
  Note Tag:      \1\    Attributes: Bold       (the tag before the note)
  Separator Bar: 2.000 in
  Renumber?: No
  Position of Notes: Bottom of Page

Levelnote Format
  Attributes, Justification, Indent, Font and SPACING as above
  Reference Tag: \a\    Attributes: Super
  Note Tag:      \a\)   Attributes: Bold
  Separator Bar: 2.000 in
  Renumber?: No
  Note Level: 1

Endnote Format
  Attributes, Justification, Indent, Font and SPACING as above
  Reference Tag: [\1\]  Attributes: Normal
  Note Tag:      \1\.   Attributes: Normal
  Note Page Title: Endnotes
  Title Font: Headline (10.0 pitch Generic)
  Title Attributes: Bold

What the Release 1 manual says about the same settings (chapter 4, Global
Note Format; chapter 3, Creating Notes):
  - Footnotes print at the bottom of the page, level notes at the end of the
    level named by Note Level (1 means the end of each level-1 section), and
    end notes at the end of the document.
  - A tag is \1\ (numbers), \A\ or \a\ (letters), \I\ or \i\ (roman numerals),
    or any other character, printed once for the first note, twice for the
    second, three times for the third, and so on.
  - Renumber says whether the notes are numbered again on each page (for
    footnotes) or level (for level notes), or numbered one after another in
    the document.  Bottom Align (in Release 1) says whether footnotes are
    at the bottom of the page or just after the text; Release 2 calls it
    Position of Notes (Bottom of Page; the other choice was not looked at).
  - The marker decides where the reference tag is in the text and which page
    a footnote is on.  A long footnote that does not fit gives the error
    "Footnote split required".

How the settings are stored (probed: values were changed in the original,
the document was saved and the header records were read).  A record is written
only when the value is not the default.  k is 1 for the footnote panel, 2 for
the levelnote panel and 3 for the endnote panel.

  N<k>R<text>NUL   reference tag (N1R\A\, N3R<\A\>)
  N<k>N<text>NUL   note tag (N1N(\i\))
  N<k>X<mask>      attributes of the reference tag (N1X20)
  N<k>Y<mask>      attributes of the note tag (N1Y5)
  N<k>SL<n>        line spacing in hundredths (N1SL125 is 1.25)
  N<k>ST<n>        block spacing; N<k>SN<n> note spacing (N1ST175, N1SN225)
  NS<k><n>         separator bar in thousandths of an inch (NS13500 is 3.500;
                   footnotes and levelnotes)
  NZ<k><0|1>       Renumber (NZ11 is Yes)
  NB<0|1>          Position of Notes: 0 After Text, 1 Bottom of Page (default)
  NL<n>            Note Level of levelnotes
  NE<text>NUL      Note Page Title of the endnotes (NENotes Here)
  NT<n>            Primary Note Type, 2 levelnotes, 3 endnotes
  N1F...           a font (seen in a sample, N1FF10; not probed)

The remaining settings of a panel (probed by changing each one in the Footnote,
Levelnote and Endnote panels and reading the saved file):

  N<k>FA<mask>   Attributes of the notes (the top row of the panel)
  N<k>FJ<n>      Justification: 0 Left, 1 Right, 2 Center, 3 Even (N1FJ3)
  N<k>FI<n>      Indent: 0 None, 1 First, 2 All but First, 3 All (N1FI2)
  N<k>FF<n>      Font, as a number (N1FF5; Headline 1, Text 2, Header/Footer
                 3, Font 4 to Font 20 are 4 to 20; N1FF10 is in a sample)

The attribute masks of the original, probed one by one (a field holds the sum
of the entries that are ticked, so Italics and Underscore All is 516):

    1 Bold                    2 Underscore Words      4 Italics
    8 Subscript              16 Superscript          32 Strikethrough
   64 Printer Specific 1    128 not found (no entry of the list)
  256 Printer Specific 2    512 Underscore All     1024 Double Underscore Words
 2048 Small Caps           4096 Double Underscore All

These masks are those of the records of the header.  The text of a block
(the 0x12 attribute token) was said to use 16 for underscored words
(DOC-FORMAT.txt); with the masks above that would be Superscript, so the
token may use other bits or that old inference may be wrong: to check.
Choices of Position of Notes: Bottom of Page and After Text.  The Pos field
(the ruler for the tab stops of notes) and the title font and the title
attributes of the Endnote panel were not probed.

The Note panel of the original (probed) has two fields: Note (the text, "..."
opens the editor) and Type, a list of four: [Footnote] (the first entry, in
brackets, names the Primary Note Type of the document: it is [Levelnote] when
NT2), Footnote, Levelnote and Endnote.  A note that has [..] chosen has no
type record and takes the Primary Note Type; the others are stored as the
field 1 of the marker: 11 for Footnote (read back from a saved file), and 12
and 13 for Levelnote and Endnote (the numbers follow the NT records; they
were not read back from the original).  Alt-F12 on a marker opens the panel
of that marker in Strata; that is the decision of the project owner, so the
key that does it in the original does not matter.

The graphic Preview of the original (display driver and printer as in
MARKERS.txt, Keycap) draws a footnote at the bottom of the page of its mark: a
short rule (the Separator Bar, about two inches) and under it the note tag and
the text in a smaller type.  Preview of the levelnotes and endnotes was not
read closely (the endnote page and the end of the level are on later pages).

Still not probed: the records of Pos, Attributes, Justification, Indent and
Font of the three panels, and the Title Font and Title Attributes of the
Endnote panel (the panels were changed only in the fields listed above).

What Strata does with it (done)
  - Global, Marker has the three panels (PgDn after the counters; they show
    spacing, tags, attributes, separator, Renumber, Position of Notes, Note
    Level and Note Page Title) and writes the records above.
  - Note.type is a field of the Note panel (Footnote, Levelnote, Endnote).
  - Each note is numbered among the notes of its kind in the order of the
    text.  The mark is made from the Reference Tag (\1\, \a\, \A\, \i\,
    \I\, or text repeated once, twice, three times); the number of the note
    tag is the same.  In a printout the mark replaces the marker.
  - The text printout prints a footnote after the block that has its mark,
    a levelnote after the section at the Note Level, and the endnotes at the
    end under the Note Page Title.  Renumber for levelnotes starts at 1 in
    each such list; footnotes cannot be renumbered per page without pages,
    which is for the LaTeX printing.
  - The display mode of a Note stays the diamond; the label in Long mode is
    NOTE "text" as in the original.
