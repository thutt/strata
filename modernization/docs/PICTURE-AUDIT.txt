THE AUDIT OF THE PICTURES OF THE REFERENCE MANUAL

Worklist item: "Pictures of the reference manual must not be copies".
Made on 2026-11-16 by reading only.  No picture, program, manual source or
original file was changed.  The script that repeats the text part of the
audit is manual/checkpicscopies.py.


1. THE RESULT IN SHORT

  - No picture is a copy of a figure of the original manuals, and none can
    be: all 158 pictures are made by Strata, and 156 of them are coloured
    screens of a kind that a scan of a 1990s manual cannot hold (section 3.1).
  - No picture shows text, a document, a name or a file name of the
    original product.  The documents that the pictures show were written for
    Strata, and they share with the original manuals and the 55 original
    sample documents only five short runs of ordinary English words
    (section 3.2).  The original's sample people and places (Centennial
    Park, Central City, CNS, CNSDRAFT.DOC, CNSMEMO.DOC) are in none of our
    sources.
  - The screens look like the original's in layout and in the labels of
    menus and panels, because Strata is a reimplementation and the owner
    chose that.  In the three pairs that were put side by side the entries,
    the text and the values differ (section 3.3).
  - One similarity is in the STRUCTURE of the text, not in a picture: the
    order of the headings of chapter 1 (Text, Block, Column, Section,
    Unstructured and Structured Documents) and of chapter 2 (Templates,
    Creating Templates, Using Templates) is the order of the original's
    table of contents (section 3.4).
  - NOT proved by this audit: that the cursor place, the highlight and the
    values typed into panels differ in every one of the pictures; that the
    order of the figures of a subject differs from the original's in every
    chapter.  The original has no list of figures and no captions (it is a
    scan), so these were checked by looking at three pairs, not for all.
  - Per picture: clean 149, generic words
    4, heading order 2, printed pages 3 (section 5).


2. WHAT WAS DONE

  The original material: Reference.pdf (418 pages), Getting productive.pdf
  (196), Getting started.pdf (19) and Quick reference.pdf (13), read from
  documentation/original/manuals-1.0 (they are scans of pages of about 2100
  by 2980 pixels with a text layer made by character recognition, which is
  noisy: it reads "developrnen1" for "development"), and the 55 files of the
  original product's sample folder, which are tracked in this repository as
  lotus-manuscript/sample.

  1. The files.  The figures are inside the page scans, not separate files
     (pdfimages lists one image of a page each, plus 168 images of 1 by 1
     pixel), so a comparison of picture files with figure files cannot be
     made, and a search for a picture inside a scan was not made (too
     slow without an image library).  In its place: how each picture is
     made; whether a picture could be a scan; the sizes and colours.
  2. The text.  The words of the sample documents of the manual (their
     sources manual/samples/src/*.src and the built documents chars.doc,
     spelldemo.doc, markers-all.doc, latexdemo.doc and
     tests/data/print_errors.doc), the typed values of every scene, and the
     names of the original's sample documents were compared with the words
     of the original manuals and of the original sample documents: runs of
     5 words, and of 4 words, with the format records of the document files
     left out.
  3. The sequence.  The headings of each chapter of the manual in the order
     of manual/src/master.txt were compared with the 206 entries of the
     original's table of contents: the longest run of headings in the same
     order, and the length of the longest common subsequence.  The figures
     under the matching headings are named.
  4. The sample documents.  Where each one comes from (git history, the
     contents), and whether the folders of the original's samples and the
     owner's own sample.docs are used by a picture.
  5. Looking.  The Document Manager (original page 22), the Append command
     (page 69) and the Section figure (page 19) were put next to the
     pictures view-docmgr, edit-a-append and view-structured.


3. THE RESULTS

3.1 The files

  - Every picture but three is made by a scene of manual/shots.py or of
    manual/scenes/*.py: 155 scenes, 155 pictures.  The other three are
    pages that the Printer prints from Strata documents: print-errlog
    (manual/scenes/errlog-pdf.sh), print-failed-page (failed-pdf.sh) and
    appchar-print (the page printed from chars.doc; no script in the tree
    makes it: it was added with the chapter, commit e66d1054, and a script
    like the other two should make it).
  - The picture check of the last retake (155 compared, 155 the same, 0
    flagged, 2026-11-16) shows that the scenes remake the pictures.  This
    audit did not run it again.
  - Of the 158 pictures 156 are coloured (blue and yellow screens, grey
    bars, a coloured logo); the only grey ones are appchar-print (a printed
    page) and str-variables-panel (a small panel).  The scans of the original
    are black on white, so a picture that is a crop of a scan would be
    grey with halftone noise.  141 of the pictures are exactly 800 pixels
    wide (the width of the window of the program), the others 640 to 850:
    the sizes of windows and of printed pages, not the sizes of scanned
    figures.
  - The Tutorial uses 13 of the pictures and Getting Started 4; the cheat
    sheets none.  No page uses a picture that is not in doc/pictures, and no
    picture of the folder is unused.

3.2 The text

  Runs of 5 words found in both a sample document of the manual and the
  original material (all five are ordinary English, in no case a sentence of
  the original reused):
    report.src    "three parts and each part"        Getting productive
    showcase.src  "at the foot of the"               Getting productive
    showcase.src  "is printed at the foot"           Getting productive
    showcase.src  "printed at the foot of"           Getting productive
    showcase.src  "a reference to another section"   Reference
  (our text: "divided into three parts, and each part into short sections";
  "a footnote ... is printed at the foot of the page"; "a reference to
  another section".  The original: "a chapter might have three parts and
  each part might be made up of several sections"; "the footnote is printed
  at the foot of the appropriate page"; "Use Cross-Reference to enter a
  reference to another section of a structured document".  They describe
  the same features of the product in the words that anyone would use.)
  With runs of 4 words seven more such phrases are found ("a line of
  text", "on a new page", "a b c d" as the letters of a formula and the
  like).  The built documents
  chars.doc, spelldemo.doc, latexdemo.doc, print_errors.doc, eqbad.doc,
  eqdemo.doc and eqgallery.doc, memo.src and the equation sources share no
  run of 5 words.  The headlines of the samples (Quarterly report, Results,
  Sales, By region, By product, Costs, People, Plan, Goals, Schedule, Risks,
  Appendix; Memo, What changes, What to do, Questions) are not in the
  original's text as a sequence; "What to do" and "The last page" are
  common phrases.
  The names of the original's samples (Centennial, Central City, Woodward,
  Bonner, Simons, CNS) are in none of our sources, scenes, tests/data
  documents or pictures' sources.
  The values that the scenes type: only "First block", "Second block"
  (edit-a-blocks) and "letter" (file-new-type) are words of the original
  text, and they are ordinary words.  The file name memo.doc (the main
  sample) is not a file name of the original's figures: the original uses
  CNSMEMO.DOC and CNSDRAFT.DOC; "memo.doc" occurs only inside them.

3.3 Looking at three pairs

  - Document Manager.  Original page 22: a menu of ten entries (Edit
    Document, Print Document, Preview Document, Check Document Spelling,
    Maintain Spelling Dictionary, Compare Document Versions, Import from
    1-2-3/Symphony File, File Conversion Utilities, Setup, Exit).  Ours
    (view-docmgr): five entries (Edit / Preview / Print Document, Maintain
    Dictionaries, Compare Document Versions, Setup, Quit) on a blue screen.
    Same subject; the entries and the text differ.
  - Append.  Original page 69: two strips of the screen with the document
    CNSDRAFT.DOC and its "Pedestrian and Traffic Surveys" headline.  Ours
    (edit-a-append): the Append menu open over the quarterly report sample.
    Same command; a different document, a different view.
  - Section.  Original page 19: the screen of CNSDRAFT.DOC with callouts
    (Section, Section indicator, Headline block, Text block).  Ours
    (view-structured): the outline of the quarterly report sample, without
    callouts.  Same subject; different content.
  In all three the layout of the screen (status line at the top, the menu
  box, the headline marks) is the original's: the owner's decision.

  Only chapter 1 has four or more consecutive headings in the original's

  equal run is 2 because the original's list is mixed with concepts.

3.5 The sample documents

  Written for Strata (the git history shows each was added by this project,
  between 2026-11-13 and 2026-11-15; no run of words, name or format settings of
  an original document is in them):
    manual/samples/src/memo.src, report.src, showcase.src, eqdemo.src,
    eqbad.src, eqgallery.src (made into documents by manual/build.sh);
    manual/samples/chars.doc, spelldemo.doc, markers-all.doc, latexdemo.doc
    (committed documents); tests/data/print_errors.doc (the Error Log test
    document).
  Material of the original product: the 55 files of lotus-manuscript/sample
  (cns2memo.doc, cns5drft.doc, cns10pp.doc, sample1.doc ... and the
  .set, .mac, .lib, .gmf, .pic, .wk1 files) are tracked in this repository.
  No scene, no sample and no picture of the manual uses them.  The tests use
  them (the variable DOCSRC of the Makefile: the round trip and the print
  tests of tests/*.py): an open question for the owner (section 7).
  The owner's own sample.docs (940408a.doc ... twain.doc) are not tracked
  and are used by no picture; tests/edtest.c and tests/preview.py use
  twain.doc as a long document for timing.


4. WHAT WAS NOT DONE

  The figures of the original were not listed or counted: the pages have no
  captions, and a rough detector of the shaded screen strips found 8 strips
  on a page that has 2, so its count (571 over 418 pages) was not used.  A
  picture was not searched for inside a scan.  The cursor place, the
  highlight and the values typed into panels were not compared for each of
  the pictures, only for the three pairs above.  The provenance table that
  the worklist item asks for (manual/pictures.txt: for each picture the
  scene, the command, the document, the program and its build) was not
  written; the table in section 5 gives the scene document and a verdict.
  The pictures were not remade for this audit.

6. RECOMMENDATIONS

  1. No picture must be retaken because of this audit.  No picture copies a
     figure, and none shows text, a name or a document of the original.
  2. The four pictures with generic words typed (edit-a-blocks: "First
     block" and "Second block"; file-new-type, str-newfile-panel and
     str-newfile-type: "letter") may be retaken with other words if the
     owner wants no word at all in common; the words are ordinary and the
     value of the change is small.
  3. The headings of chapter 1 (Text, Block, Column, Section, Unstructured
     and Structured Documents) and of chapter 2 (Templates, Creating
     Templates, Using Templates) follow the original's order.  If the owner
     wants more distance, change the order or the titles in the sources
     view*.src and file*.src; the pictures view-structured and file-new
     stay valid.  This is the only sequence similarity that was found.
  4. Make a script for appchar-print, like errlog-pdf.sh, so that every
     picture of the manual has a recipe.
  5. Write the provenance table manual/pictures.txt that the worklist item
     asks for: the scene and the document of each picture are in section 5;
     the program and the build come from manual/shots.py.
  6. Ask the owner to look at the panel pictures once (str-*, format-* and
     file-* pictures that show typed values), because a machine comparison
     of panel values was not made.
  7. Run manual/checkpicscopies.py again when a sample document is added or
     changed: python3 -I manual/checkpicscopies.py FOLDER, where FOLDER holds
     the text of the original manuals (*.txt made by pdftotext -layout) and
     the original sample documents in a folder named sample.  It exits with
     1 when it finds a run; the five phrases of section 3.2 are known and
     are ordinary English.


7. QUESTIONS FOR THE OWNER

  - The original product's own sample documents (55 files in
    lotus-manuscript/sample: cns2memo.doc, cns5drft.doc, sample1.doc ...)
    are tracked in this repository and the tests use them.  No picture uses
    them.  Before anything is shipped, should the tests use only documents
    written for Strata?  (Decided here: no change.)
  - Should the order of the headings of chapters 1 and 2 be changed
    (recommendation 3)?  (Decided here: no change.)


8. HOW SURE EACH VERDICT IS

  - No file copy: certain.  Five facts agree: every picture is made by a
    scene or by the Printer; the scenes remake them identically; 156 of 158
    are coloured and the original's scans are not; the sizes are those of
    windows; the original has no separate figure files.
  - No copied text, document or name: high.  The original sample documents
    were read exactly (strings of the files); the original manuals were read
    through a character recognition layer that misreads some words, so a
    sentence of the original that was misread could have been missed.  The
    distinctive names were looked for as well as runs of words, and none was
    found, which makes a copy that the runs missed very unlikely.
  - Panel values, cursor places and highlights: low to medium.  Checked by
    eye in three pairs, and by the typed values of the scenes (none of them
    is a distinctive value of the original).  The defaults of the panels are
    the product's own defaults and equal the original's where the product is
    the same.
  - The sequence of the figures: medium.  Compared by headings only; the
    figures of the original could not be listed.



9. FOLLOW-UP (2026-11-17)

  What the recommendations of section 6 became:

  - Recommendation 2 (the generic words).  The scenes that typed ordinary
    words now type other words that neither the original manuals nor the
    original sample documents hold: edit-a-blocks types "Tides rise at dusk",
    "Gulls follow the boats" and "beyond the pier" (the picture was taken
    again); file-new-type, str-newfile-type and str-newfile-panel type the
    name "harbor" instead of "letter".  The typed name is not visible in
    those three pictures (the New File question and panel show no name), so
    the pictures did not change.
  - Recommendation 3 (the order of the headings).  Done: the titles of
    chapter 1 are "What text is", "What a block is", "What a column is",
    "What a section is" and "Documents with sections and documents without";
    in chapter 2 the subsections of Templates are "Making a template file"
    and "Starting a document from a template".  The labels did not change, so
    the cross-references follow.  The longest run of headings in the order of
    the original is now 1 in both chapters.
  - Recommendation 4 (appchar-print).  manual/scenes/appchar-pdf.sh prints
    chars.doc and cuts the first page at 90 dots to the inch; the picture
    that it makes differs from the picture of the manual by 8 pixels of shape.
  - Recommendation 5 (the provenance table).  manual/pictures.txt has one
    line for every picture (165) with the scene or script, the program, the
    document, the date of the last retake and how to make it again;
    build.sh check-pictable (part of build.sh check) fails when a picture and
    a line do not belong together.
  - Also: the five pictures of Setup (str-setup, spell-str-setup,
    str-setup-panel, str-setup-browse, str-setup-scrolled) no longer show the
    build folder and the folder of the samples of the machine.  They are made
    through scenes/neutral-str.sh, which shows neutral paths:
    /home/writer/documents, /usr/local/strata/stred and
    /usr/local/strata/strata.hlp (bwrap makes a private view of the file
    system; nothing on the machine is changed).  The masks of checkpics.allow
    that left the build folder out are gone.
  - Not done: the pictures that show the folder of the samples in other
    panels (the Edit panel and the file panels show /tmp/strata-docs, and
    print-failed-message shows the folder of the document in its message).
    They show a temporary folder, not a private one; use scenes/neutral-str.sh
    as the model if the owner wants them neutral too.
