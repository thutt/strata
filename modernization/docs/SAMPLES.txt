The tests, the manual tools and the scripts used to take their input
documents from the 55 sample files of the original product (the directory
lotus-manuscript/sample at the top of the repository).  They now use
documents that were made for Strata: the directory tests/samples, which
the program tests/mksamples.py writes.  Every word of the text of the new
documents was written for them: a community orchard on a hill, its plan,
its brief, its yearly report, a utility that prints tide tables, and the
letters and memos of the club.  The new documents have the features that
the tests need; the layout of each one is a design of its own.

The originals are still in the repository.  They are not used any more.
To remove them for a release:

    git rm -r lotus-manuscript/sample

and run "strata-build test" and "strata-build smoke": nothing changes.  The
test tests/samplesguard.py (part of "strata-build test") fails when a file
under tests/, manual/ or scripts/ names an original file or the directory,
and runs the unit tools with the original directory hidden (a private view
made with bwrap), so that the guard also proves that the originals are not
read.

The variable DOCSRC names the directory of the sample documents; it
defaults to tests/samples (Makefile, tests).  Set it to another directory
to run the tests on other documents.  The documents of the other set,
sample.docs (twain.doc and others, not in the repository), are used only
where a test finds them and skips the check otherwise.


The new documents
-----------------

  plan-title-table.doc     (replaces the original cns10pp.doc)
    A structured document of three sections.  A title section with a
    headline, a bordered block of two centred lines (CB255), four blocks
    of the named tab format "tab list" with tab markers (the tab stops 24
    and 49), a team list with a bold label and tabs, and, below it, a
    section "Neighbours" with a text block (BC1) and a table of four rows
    of two columns with borders, then the sections "Planting" and "Costs
    and Grants".  25 items.

  brief-long.doc           (replaces cns5drft.doc)
    A structured brief of three levels: "Aims and Approach" with the
    subsection "Land and Layout" that has four subsections, and "Work
    Plan" with three stages that have bulleted lists (blocks with BFI2,
    BFT3,6, BL5 and BSB150 and the bullet marker), then three sections
    of text.  About six kilobytes, 73 items, a few printed pages.

  brief-parts.doc          (replaces cns7prop.doc)
    The brief with a title page of three blocks (BT, BB and BSB records)
    and the section tag format of level one, "Part I." (record F1G).

  manual-numbered.doc      (replaces scr.doc)
    A manual of a utility, with the automatic numbers on: "1 The Tide
    Table Utility", "1.1 Command Line Options" and the options "1.1.1
    Help" to "1.1.6 Mode=nn", then "1.2 Files" with two subsections.

  report-plain.doc         (replaces music.doc)
    A report with no automatic numbers (FLA0), a table of four rows of
    three columns with borders and a bulleted list.

  memo-date-table.doc      (replaces sample2.doc)
    An unstructured memo: a centred bold title, tab lines for To, From,
    Date (with a date marker) and Subject, text and a table with borders.

  letter-tabs.doc          (replaces sample1.doc)
    An unstructured letter with a date marker, an address, a list with a
    tab list (BFT9,39) and a bulleted line.

  article-footer.doc       (replaces sample3.doc)
    A short structured article with the page number marker in the footer
    (record PFB), a title section with two lines and three sections.

  reference-long.doc       (replaces pdupdate.doc)
    A structured reference of about 40 kilobytes and 488 items: five
    parts with four or five entries each; used as a long seed of the fuzz
    test.

  names.mac                (replaces guide.mac)
    A macro set: Alt-N types the club name, Alt-Y a closing line.

  boiler.lib               (replaces guide.lib)
    A library of three entries: Notice, Numbered List and Picking Table.

  orchard.set              (replaces proposal.set)
    A template with a description, typeface records (DF1, DF4, DF5) and a
    title block.

Every document loads and is written back byte for byte (roundtrip), and
the tab markers of every document hold the number of fill characters that
the Editor computes (tabcheck: no mismatch; the old limit of eleven known
mismatches of scr.doc is gone).


Where each original file was used
---------------------------------

  cns10pp.doc    smoke.py (about thirty places: the first screen, the tab
                 alignment, the outline keys, Case, Math, print, preview,
                 export, compare, backups, the library, the Setup and the
                 other panels), xsmoke.py, preview.py, printpdf.py,
                 termstate.py, nbsp.py, utf8s0.py, latexmarker.py,
                 xlatex.py, fuzz.py, edtest.c (attributes, deleting a
                 whole section, retrieve).  Features: a title page with a
                 bold headline and a bordered block, a named tab format
                 with tab markers, a table, a three-level outline.
  cns5drft.doc   smoke.py (print Draft, double spaced), printpdf.py (the
                 command line, --double-spaced), edtest.c (the main block:
                 navigation, typing, sections, search, replace, cut and
                 paste of a section, the no-break space), fuzz.py.
                 Features: a long structured text of three levels with
                 bulleted lists.
  cns7prop.doc   smoke.py (the section tag format "Part I.").
  scr.doc        smoke.py (the numbered section tags), tabcheck.c (known
                 mismatches), fuzz.py.
  music.doc      smoke.py (a document with FLA0), fuzz.py.
  guide.lib      smoke.py (retrieve and paste a library entry, the initial
                 library).
  guide.mac      smoke.py (retrieve and run a macro set, the initial macro
                 set), backuptest.c (a file name in a settings test).
  proposal.set   smoke.py (Save and Retrieve of global settings),
                 edtest.c (the Retrieve commands).
  sample1.doc, sample2.doc, sample3.doc, pdupdate.doc
                 fuzz.py (seeds).
  memo.set       named as an example in two pages of the manual (file1.src
                 and file3.src); the manual does not use the file.
  The whole directory: the Makefile ran roundtrip, tabcheck, loadtest and
                 edtest on it and passed it to tmpclean.py and the test
                 secnum.py; printtest.sh printed every document.
  The other 31 files (pictures, graphs, spreadsheets, fonts and so on, the
                 other documents of the set) were not used by name.


The sample business letters
---------------------------

  samples/letters/         (16 files: 8 letters, each on US Letter paper,
                           NAME-us.doc, and on A4 paper, NAME-a4.doc)
    Finished business letters that use the letter style of the templates
    letter-us and letter-a4: the named formats Sender, Date, Recipient,
    Salutation, Body, Closing, Signature, Enclosure, Copy and Postscript,
    with the records that the templates give them.  A letter adds only the
    named formats Subject, Table head and Table row.  No block of a letter
    has a format that is set by hand.  The program tests/mkletters.py writes
    them:

        python3 -I -B tests/mkletters.py samples/letters

    The letters are enquiry, quotation (with a table that has the borders of
    the manual tables), complaint-reply, complaint-to-utility (a customer's
    complaint to the made-up company Pathetic Gas & Deceptive: the name has
    an ampersand that is printed as it is), order-confirmation, enclosures
    (three encl lines and two cc lines), two-pages (the head of the second
    page names the recipient, the date and the page) and sans-serif (font 2
    is named Text (sans) and is the text font, record FTF2).  The dates are
    text, so the files are the same bytes on every run.  The A4 letters keep
    the sender at three lines and the recipient at four, so that the
    recipient is in the window of a DL window envelope (docs/ENVELOPES.txt).

    The folder is installed by scripts/strata-install in
    share/strata/samples/letters.  tests/letters.py (part of the smoke)
    checks the letters; tests/installtest.py checks the install.  Chapter 2
    of the Reference Manual, New, has the section "Sample letters".
