TABLES IN THE SUBLEVELS OF A STRUCTURED DOCUMENT: THE ORIGINAL AND STRATA
=======================================================================

Status: a probe, and one fix of the Printer (src/latex.c).  The probe was run
on 2026-11-22.  Every statement is marked:

  [tried]     seen in the original or in Strata during this probe
  [repo]      read in this repository (code, tests, docs)
  [not tried] not looked at; said so

The question (owner): a printed structured document indents its sublevels,
which is right; but the tables in the sublevels printed over the whole width
of the page.  Is that how the original worked?

THE SHORT ANSWER
----------------

No.  The original indents a table together with the text of its sublevel:
the table starts at the left margin of its level and ends at the right margin
of the page, so a table in a deeper level is not wider, only its last column
is narrower.  It does the same for a boxed paragraph and for a picture.
[tried]  Strata did that for boxed paragraphs and pictures already, but
printed a table from the left margin of the page (and ended it at the right
margin of the level minus the indent, so it was also shifted to the left of
its own text).  [tried]  That was a fault of the Printer and is fixed
(section 5).

1. HOW THE PROBE WAS MADE
-------------------------

1.1 The original [tried]

  The original ran unmodified in DOSBox-X on an Xvfb display of its own, on a
  scratch copy of the installation media (never in the source tree); keys by
  xdotool, screens by xwd.  Two print drivers were used, both sent to a file
  by the parallel port of the emulator:
    - the Generic driver (plain text: positions of characters, no borders);
    - the Apple LaserWriter driver (PostScript, with borders and pictures),
      chosen in Setup (Primary Print Driver).  The PostScript was turned into
      a PDF with ghostscript and measured like a PDF of Strata.
  The Editor of the original was run too, to see its own screen.

1.2 The probe documents [tried]

  docs/probe/tabprobe.src is written in the source format of manual/
  mkmanual.py (python3 -I -B manual/mkmanual.py tabprobe.src tabprobe.doc):
  sections of three levels (1, 1.1, 1.1.1), each with a text block and a table
  of three columns (12 12 12 cells wide); the table has a header row with a
  double line at its bottom.  docs/probe/tabprobe.doc is the result.  The
  original opened it and printed it at the first try.
  docs/probe/tables-boxprobe-orig.doc has the same three levels with a boxed
  paragraph (a Note) and a Picture marker (a file of the original's own
  sample disk, CD2.GMF) in each level.  It is made by docs/probe/
  mkorigbox.py from the generator's output: the generator writes a record
  for the numbering of captions (ZNP3) when the document has figures, and the
  original refuses a document that has it ("Invalid file"), so the picture
  blocks are put in by the script.  docs/probe/boxprobe-strata.src is the same
  document for Strata (a PNG picture, a caption).
  Real documents: the sample cns5drft.doc of the original's disks (6 nested
  sections, tables with borders) printed with both programs.  The
  Strata documents (the Getting Started document, samples/story/
  strata-story.doc and the Reference Manual) were printed with Strata only
  (the original does not read their picture files and the numbering record).
  [tried]
  The reference of the original's own manual on this point was not read (its
  manuals are not in this copy of the repository).  [not tried]

2. WHAT THE ORIGINAL DOES [tried]
---------------------------------

2.1 The screen of the original's Editor (docs/probe/tables-orig-editor-1.png
    and -2.png): the table of level 1.1 starts under the first character of
    the text of 1.1 (a few pixels in, the width of the cell border and its
    space) and ends at the same right edge as the table of level 1; the
    columns with a stated width keep it and the last column takes what is
    left.

2.2 The Generic driver (docs/probe/tables-orig-tabprobe-generic.txt): the
    text of level 1 begins in column 11, level 2 in column 14, level 3 in
    column 17 (the characters of the file counted from 1); the first
    character of the cells of the tables begins one column to the right of
    the text of its level, in every level (12, 15, 18).

2.3 The PostScript driver (docs/probe/tables-orig-tabprobe.png and
    tables-orig-boxprobe.png): the tables, the boxed paragraphs and the
    pictures start at the left margin of their level.  Measurements follow.

3. WHAT STRATA DID BEFORE THE FIX, AND DOES NOW [tried]
-------------------------------------------------------

  The probe document was printed with the Printer (strprint FILE -o OUT.pdf),
  before the fix (docs/probe/tables-strata-tabprobe-before.png) and after
  (-after.png).  The edges are the first and the last dark pixel in the row
  of the header cells in a picture of the page at 220 dpi.

4. THE MEASUREMENTS (points from the left edge of the page; Letter paper,
   text 72 to 540; the original 1 pt less at the right because its lines are
   drawn inside the edge) [tried]
-----------------------------------------------------------------------

  The original, PostScript driver, probe document tabprobe:

     level   text margin   table left   table right   width of the text
       1        72.0          73.0         538.7           466.7
       2        93.6          94.6         538.7           445.1
       3       115.2         116.2         538.7           423.5

  Strata before the fix:

       1        72.0          72.0         539.3           467.3
       2        93.6          72.0         517.7           445.7
       3       115.2          72.0         496.1           424.1

  Strata after the fix:

       1        72.0          72.0         539.3           467.3
       2        93.6          93.6         539.3           445.7
       3       115.2         115.2         539.3           424.1

  The width of a table: in both programs the columns that have a width keep
  it and the last column (which has none in these documents) takes what is
  left up to the right margin.  A table of which all the columns have a width
  is as wide as the widths say; Strata scales all the columns down in one
  proportion when they are wider than the room that is left.  [tried for the
  first; the original with all columns given a width and a sum wider than the
  room: not tried]

  Boxed paragraph and picture (the document boxprobe), left and right edge of
  the box of the paragraph; the left end of the picture:

     level   original: box         original: picture    Strata: box
       1       72.3 to 538.7         starts at 74.9       72.0 to 539.7
       2       93.9 to 538.7         starts at 96.5       93.6 to 539.7
       3      115.5 to 538.7         (page 2)             115.2 to 539.7

  The picture of the original is put at the left of its level (a left
  aligned block); that of Strata is centred in the width of its level (the
  centre moves right by half the indent: 306 pt at level 1, 317 pt at level
  2).  Both are indented with the level; the difference is the alignment
  that the document says, not the indent.

  A real document, cns5drft, the table ESTIMATES in section 4.2 (level 2):
  the original starts it at the text of 4.2 and ends it at the right margin
  (docs/probe/tables-orig-cns5drft-p4.png).  Strata before the fix started
  it left of its text and ended it 6 pt before the right margin
  (tables-strata-cns5drft-before.png); after the fix it starts at the text
  and ends at the right margin (-after.png).  Other differences of this table
  (the row that spans the columns, the fonts, the line breaks of the cells)
  are not part of this question.

5. THE FIX (src/latex.c, put_tabhead) [repo]
--------------------------------------------

  The Printer already moved the table to the indent of its level
  (\setlength{\LTleft}{...}) and made its columns narrower by the indent.  The
  table environment was started as longtable[l], and the longtable package
  sets its left space to zero for the option l, so the indent was thrown
  away.  Now the longtable has no option: its default leaves the left and the
  right space as the Printer set them.  A table in a Keep part (set one
  tabular per row) already had the indent.
  Test: tests/tabindent.py prints the probe document tabprobe.src and reads the
  edges of the three tables from a picture; it fails with the Printer before
  the fix (6 checks).  It is in tests/smokerun.py.

6. WHAT STAYS [tried]
---------------------

  The Getting Started document (46 pages), the story sample (21 pages) and the
  Reference Manual (742 pages) print with the same number of pages before and
  after the fix; no word of any of them goes past the right margin that did
  not before (the Reference Manual has 6 such words in both, on its sheets
  that are wider than the page; the Getting Started document has one word of a
  table, ~/.tmux.conf, that touches the right border of its cell, 0.8 pt past
  the margin, where before the whole table stood 25 px too far left).  The
  manual text on the indent of tables is in the chapter on the format
  panels (Indent per Level).

7. NOT TRIED
------------

  - A page printer driver other than Apple LaserWriter (PCL).
  - A table in the original whose columns all have widths that add up to
    more than the room of a deep level.
  - The sorted order of the print and the contents of the original with
    tables in them.
