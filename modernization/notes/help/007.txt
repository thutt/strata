
---------------------------- Check Document Spelling ---------------------------

Check Document Spelling can be accessed from the Document Manager or from 
Menu (F10) Tools Spell in the editor.  From within the editor, Check Document 
Spelling verifies the spelling of an entire document (excluding index entries)
or individual blocks, sections, or words.
