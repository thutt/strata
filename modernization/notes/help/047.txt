
------------------------------------- Move -------------------------------------

Move (F7) moves highlighted columns, blocks, or sections and changes section 
levels within a structured document. All notes, index entries, and cross-
references move with the highlighted element.

Move columns within current block.     Move sections, including subsections, to
(To move columns that span blocks,     another location or level.  (This option
use the Column and Table Editor.)      is available only in Structured mode.)

                          +--------------------------+
                          | Column   Block   Section |
                          +--------------------------+
                                       |
                          Move blocks (within the current
                          section in a structured document).
--Procedure---------------------------------------------------------------------

 Press Move and select Column, Block, or Section.
 Highlight the columns, blocks, or sections to move and press Enter.
 Move the highlighted columns, blocks, or sections and press Enter.
