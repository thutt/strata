
------------------------------- Move Section Keys ------------------------------

After highlighting the sections to move, use the following keys to move the 
sections:

  Up, Down         Up or down one section 
  Right, Left      Left or right (down or up) one level
  PgUp, PgDn       Up or down to the next section at the same level
  Home, End        To the first or last section at the same level
  Ctrl Left        Subsections of the current section left (up) one level
  Ctrl Right       Subsequent sections at the same level right (down) one level
  Ctrl [           Current section left (up) one level
  Ctrl ]           Current section right (down) one level



Use the following keys to change the highlighted region of sections:

  Shift Up, Shift Down        Up or down to the next section 
  Shift Right, Shift Left     Left (up) or right (down) one level
  Shift PgUp, Shift PgDn      Up or down to the next section  at the same level
