
-------------------------------- Error Messages --------------------------------

-- Background color must be different from all foreground colors -- 

The background color must be different from all of the foreground colors. Change
either the foreground or background colors and continue with Setup.

-- Block is too large to print -- 

The block being printed is larger than can be stored in memory. Check for margin
indent or paper size problems or break into smaller blocks and reprint.

-- Block width exceeds maximum of 210 ruler units --

The width of the block has exceeded the maximum 210 ruler units.  Use the 
Column and Table Editor to change column widths.

-- Bottom margin is smaller than minimum required by printer --

The printer requires a larger bottom margin than is specified on the Page Layout
panels. Edit the bottom margin selection and reprint the document.
