
-------------------------------- Error Messages --------------------------------

-- Document printer not recognized -- 

The printer specified for the document is not supported by your current release
of Manuscript.  The document was created or edited in a more recent release 
with expanded printer support.

-- Document upgrade required (<filename>) -- 

The document being printed or a document used by the document being printed
(such as an Include file) must be upgraded to Release 2 for printing to
continue.  The document upgrade is only for the current printing session and
will not affect the disk version of the file.

-- Documents cannot be compared; less than 50% of words match --

Less than 50% of the text of the two files matches, so the files cannot be
compared. Split the input files into smaller files and compare the corresponding
smaller pieces of the two files.
