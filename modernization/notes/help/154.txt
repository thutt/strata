
--------------------------------- Justification --------------------------------

Justification settings made on the local column or block (F6) and global format
(F10 Format) panels affect the way text aligns with the margins when you print
or preview a document.

Left:   Aligns text with the left margin, leaving the right margin ragged.

Right:  Aligns text with the right margin, leaving the left margin ragged.

Center: Centers text between the margins.

Even:   Aligns text with both the left and right margins. Even justification
        is not ordinarily displayed on the screen, but is formatted during
        final printing. You can display even justification on the screen by 
        changing the Display Even Justification setting on the Menu (F10) 
        Global Options panel to Yes.
