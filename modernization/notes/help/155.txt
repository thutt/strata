
---------------------------------- Line Indents --------------------------------


Indent settings on the local column or block (F6) and global format (F10 Format)
panels control which lines begin at the first tab or, if a tab is not set, 
at the sixth ruler position.


None:          Does not indent any lines of text.

First:         Indents the first line of text only.

All but First: Indents all lines of text except the first (a hanging indent).

All:           Indents all lines of text.
