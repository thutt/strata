
----------------------------- Date and Time Formats ----------------------------

To change the global Date or Time marker format, select Menu (F10) Global 
Marker, move the cursor to the Date Format or Time Format prompt, press Space 
Bar, and type the desired format using the following symbols:

Date Formats                         Time Formats
---------------------------------    -------------------------------------------
d      one- or two-digit day         h     one- or two-digit hour (12 hour form)
dd     two-digit day                 hh    two-digit hour (12 hour form)
day    day spelled out               h24   one- or two-digit hour (24 hour form)
m      one- or two-digit month       hh24  two-digit hour (24 hour form)
mm     two-digit month               m     one- or two-digit minute
mon    three-letter month abbrev.    mm    two-digit minute
month  month spelled out             s     one- or two-digit second
yy     two-digit year                ss    two-digit second
year   four-digit year               am/pm prints appropriate indicator

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Date and Time markers insert the current date and time in a document using 
the format specified on the Marker Format panel.  To override the global format,
use the symbols above in individual Date and Time markers.
