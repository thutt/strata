
----------------- Setting the Amount of Space to Indent Levels -----------------

The Indent per Level and Indent to Next Level prompts on the Menu (F10) Global 
Format and Level Format panels set the amount of space to indent or outdent 
levels. You can specify different indents for each level.

Example:      Indent per level: 3          (Global Format panel)
              Indent to Next Level: 10     (Level 3 Format panel)

 :::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::
 :: 1 Level 1 heading
 ::    ---------------------------------------------------------------------
 ::    1.1 Level 2 heading
 ::       ------------------------------------------------------------------
 ::       1.1.1 Level 3 heading
 ::                 --------------------------------------------------------
 ::                 1.1.1.1 Level 4 heading
 ::                    -----------------------------------------------------
 ::                    1.1.1.1.1 Level 5 heading

All level headlines, except level 4, are indented 3 units from the the preceding
level; level 4 headings are indented 10 units from level 3.
