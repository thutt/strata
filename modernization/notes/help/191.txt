
----------------------------- Cursor-Movement Keys -----------------------------

Left/Right    Left/right one character
Up/Down       Up/down one line
PgUp/PgDn     Up/down approximately one-half screen
Home          Beginning of line
End           End of line

Ctrl Left     Left one word
Ctrl Right    Right one word
Ctrl Home     Beginning of current column or block
Ctrl End      End of current column or block

Shift Left    Left one column or up one level
Shift Right   Right one column or down one level
Shift Up      Up to the current section's headline block
Shift Down    Down to the next section's headline block
Shift PgUp    Up one section at the same level (structured documents only)
Shift PgDn    Down one section on the same level (structured documents only)
Shift Home    Beginning of document
Shift End     End of document
