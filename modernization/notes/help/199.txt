
------------------------------------ Overtype ----------------------------------

Overtype (Alt F5) is a toggle that changes how characters are entered on the 
edit screen.  Normally, characters are inserted as you type, moving any 
characters that follow to the right.  With Overtype, each character you type 
replaces the character at the cursor position.



# Overtype is especially useful for long portions of text that need changes 
  throughout.


# When you press Overtype, [OT] appears in the lower-right corner of the 
  Status line. 


# To turn off overtype, press Overtype again.
