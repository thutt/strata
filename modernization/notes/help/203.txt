
--------------------------------- Ruler Spacing --------------------------------

Ruler spacing settings made on the local format (F6) and global format (F10 
Global Format) panels control the size of a ruler unit when the document 
final prints. Ruler units affect the following settings: column widths, tab 
placement, left and right block indents, and level indents.

Ruler units represent either a relative distance or an absolute measurement 
from a margin.  The Ruler prompt has the following options:

Relative: Text, blocks, and columns have the same relative position in the print
          area that they have on the screen. For example, if you place a tab at
          position 40, text aligned with the tab prints halfway between the left
          and right margins of the paper (40 divided by 80 equals 1/2).

10/inch:  With any of these absolute ruler settings, each space on the ruler 
12/inch   represents a fraction of an inch. Use absolute spacing to position 
15/inch   a column a precise distance in inches from the left margin.  For
          example, if you set ruler spacing to 10/inch (10 units per inch)
          and the tabs are at 10 and 25, the tabbed text prints 1 inch and 
          2.5 inches from the left margin.
