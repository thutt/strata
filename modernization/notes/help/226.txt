
----------------------------------- Tab Types ----------------------------------

Manuscript has 6 tab types that you can set on a format panel's ruler.
+------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|*TABS.DOC      Attr: Normal         Pos: 1                   UNSTRUCTURED EDIT|
|......................................^...........,.....................#...|
+------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
  left-aligned   right-aligned     center       2,000     1.3       14 %
                                            May 2, 1988  45.534   25.3 %
                                               56,000      .99    0.25 %

Left-aligned tabs () align all text flush left with the tab.

Right-aligned tabs () align all text flush right with the tab.

Center tabs (^) center all text around the tab.

Comma tabs (,) align text or numbers on a comma.

Decimal tabs (.) align numbers on a decimal point.

Number tabs (#) align numbers on the last digit.
