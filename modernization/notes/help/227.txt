
------------------------------ Tab-fill Characters -----------------------------

Tab-fill characters fill the blank spaces between tabs. You can use any 
character as a tab fill.  Three commonly used fill characters are periods (.), 
dashes (-), and underscores (_).

--Procedure---------------------------------------------------------------------

In the text of the document
 Press Compose (Alt F1).
 Press Tab.
 Press the character to use as a tab fill character.
