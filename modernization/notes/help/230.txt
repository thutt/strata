
------------------------------------ Markers -----------------------------------
The following markers control various aspects of the printed page format.

Column Break: Inserts a manual column break into a winding-column portion of 
              text. Place the marker in its own block.

Page Break:   Inserts a page break and prints subsequent text on the next page,
              next odd page, or next even page.  

Whitespace:   Inserts white space (for example, for inserting art) at the marker
              location during final print. Crop at End of Page inserts white
              space in the amount specified or until the end of a page is 
              encountered, whichever is less. Place All on Next Page inserts
              white space in the amount specified and moves it to the next
              page if it does not fit on the current page. Float to Next page is
              the same as Place on Next Page except that it moves subsequent
              text forward to fill the space preceding the white space.

Font:         Changes the font for text from the marker to the end of the 
              current column or block or until another Font marker is 
              encountered.
