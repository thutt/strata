-------------------------------- Error Messages --------------------------------

-- Page number tag more than 20 characters -- 

Change the page number tag to be less than 20 characters.

-- Picture error (picture/equation occurs after line wraps) -- 

A picture or equation with position set to Fixed or Floating occurs after the
line of text surrounding it wrapped.  Change the position of the marker to
In text or place the marker in its own block 

-- Picture height exceeds page length -- 

The picture is too large to fit on a page.  Change the size or scale options in
the Picture marker and reprint the document.

-- Picture is too large; will be automatically scaled --

The image size specified in a Picture marker is larger than the space available
or the natural size of the picture is larger than the space available. Change 
the size and reprint the document.
