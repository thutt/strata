
-------------------------------- Error Messages --------------------------------


-- Right margin is smaller than minimum required by printer --

The printer requires a larger right margin than was specified on the Global
Page Layout panels. Edit the margin selections and reprint the document.


-- Save corrected document? -- 

Changes have been made to the document during spell checking.  Press Y to save
the changes or N to cancel the changes.


-- Save document? -- 

You have selected the With Prompt option for the Autosave prompt on the Options
panel and the document has been modified but not saved.  Press Y to save and 
continue editing or N to continue editing without saving.
