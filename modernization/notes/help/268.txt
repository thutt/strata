
-------------------------------- Error Messages --------------------------------


-- Window must be SHEET or FORM type in Worksheet marker --

The window you specified in a Worksheet marker is not a SHEET or FORM window, 
so no data can be imported. Reenter the window name or leave the name blank and
Manuscript will use the first sheet or form window in the worksheet.


-- Window not found in worksheet (window: <name>) --

The window name specified is not in the worksheet.


-- Word is too long - maximum length is <value> characters -- 

The word you are trying to look up in the thesaurus is longer than 16 characters
or the word you are trying to spell check is longer than 65 characters.  Edit
the word and retry the operation.
