
------------ Creating Custom Spelling and Hyphenation Dictionaries -------------

To create a spelling or hyphenation dictionary, just create a new document and 
enter words following these rules:

# Words must be separated by either a space, a tab, or an end-of-line character.

# For spelling dictionaries, use capitalization and punctuation only if a word
  must appear that way. Words entered in lowercase are also added with an 
  initial capital letter.  Words entered in uppercase or with an initial 
  capital letter are added exactly as typed.

# For hyphenation dictionaries, type a soft hyphen (Ctrl -) where you want to
  hyphenate the word. To prevent a word from being hyphenated, type it in the
  document without any soft hyphens.  Enter the words in lowercase (Manuscript 
  will hyphenate all cases of the word) and enter possessives when applicable.

Use Maintain Dictionaries on the Document Manager to convert the document to 
the appropriate type of dictionary.

You can also create a custom spelling dictionary by typing its name on the Check
Document Spelling panel; Manuscript creates it as you spell check a document.
