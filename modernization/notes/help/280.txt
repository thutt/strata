
----------------------------- Dictionary Language ------------------------------




Dictionary Language:  Specify the language to be used by the spell checker and
                      for hyphenation.  The dictionary language for which
                      Manuscript is initially configured is the dictionary 
                      supplied on your original Manuscript disks.  Dictionaries
                      for additional languages are available from Lotus.


Decimal Separator:    Specify the symbol to use as the decimal separator in 
                      numbers on Manuscript's panels.  For example, a two inch
                      left page margin would be entered and displayed as 2,0 
                      on the Global Page Layout panel if you have specified a
                      comma decimal separator.  The Decimal Separator is also 
                      used in the document revision number maintained by 
                      Manuscript.
