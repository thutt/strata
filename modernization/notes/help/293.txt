
---------------------------------- Change Case ---------------------------------

Use Case (Ctrl F6) to change the case of existing text to all uppercase, all
lowercase, or initial capitals.


---Procedure--------------------------------------------------------------------

 Move the cursor to the beginning         
  or end of the text to change.       +-------------- Change Case ------------+
                                      |                                       |
 Press Case (Ctrl F6).               |             v No Change               |
                                      |               UPPER                   |
 Highlight the text to change and    |               lower                   |
  press Enter.                        |               Initial                 |
                                      |                                       |
 Use Up and Down to move the cursor  +- Press Ins to Use Displayed Settings -+
  to the setting and press Space Bar
  to select the setting.

 Press Ins to accept the setting.
