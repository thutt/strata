
----------------------------- Repaginate/Clear-pages ---------------------------

Use the Menu (F10) Print Repaginate and Clear-pages commands to display or 
remove page breaks in the editor.

Repaginate:   Paginates the document and displays page breaks in the editor.

Clear-pages:  Removes page breaks that are displayed in the editor.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

# The display or removal of page breaks from the editor does not affect the 
  actual page breaks of the final printed document.

# Page breaks displayed in the editor reflect the locations of page breaks at
  the time the document was last paginated.  They do not reflect subsequent
  changes that you may have made to the document.

# To Go To (Alt F7) a page, you must have paginated the document.

# If a page break occurs within a marker, such as an Include marker, the 
  page break is displayed next to the marker.
