
------------------------------------ Thesaurus ---------------------------------

Use the Menu (F10) Tools Thesaurus command to look up the current word in the 
thesaurus.                                

Accelerator:  Ctrl T
                                       
# If the word is in the thesaurus, synonyms are displayed.

   To replace the word in the document with a synonym, highlight the new word 
    and press Enter.    
   To look up one of the synonyms in the thesaurus, highlight the word and 
    press Ctrl T.
   To leave the thesaurus without changing the document, press Esc.

# If the word is not in the thesaurus, a list of similarly spelled words is
  displayed.
  
   To look up one of the words on the list, highlight the word and press 
    Enter or Ctrl T.
   To look up a new word, simply start typing the word and press Enter.
   To leave the thesaurus without changing the document, press Esc.
