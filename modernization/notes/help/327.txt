
------------------------------- Screen Capture Format --------------------------

Use Screen Capture Format to specify global shading values and whether or
not to show the cursor on the screen capture image.  Use the Screen Capture 
marker to insert the image in a document.

---Procedure---------------------------   +------ Screen Capture Format -----+
                                          |                                  |
 Press Menu (F10) and select Global      | Show Cursor?                     |
  Marker.                                 |  Normal Video Foreground:        |
                                          |  Normal Video Background:        |
 Press Space Bar at the Screen Capture   | Reverse Video Foreground:        |
  Format prompt and enter the values.     | Reverse Video Background:        |
                                          |                                  |
 Press Ins to accept your settings.      + Press Ins Use Displayed Settings +

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Show Cursor? Select Yes to show the cursor position in the screen capture image.

Normal/Reverse Video Foreground/Background: Enter a value between 0 (black) and
     100 (white) to specify the shade of gray to use for the foreground or 
     background shading of normal or reverse video display modes.
