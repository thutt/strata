
------------------------------------ Spell Word --------------------------------

Use Menu (F10) Tools Spell Word to spell the current word.   Accelerator: Ctrl C


---Procedure--------------------------------------------------------------------

 With the cursor on the word you want
  to spell check, press Menu (F10) and        +------- Spell Word -------+
  select Tools Spell Word, or press Ctrl C.   |                          |
  The Spell Word panel will display whether   |           panl           |
  the word is spelled correctly or not.       | Not found in dictionary  |
                                              |                          |
 If the word is spelled correctly, press     | Suggested alternatives:  |
  any key to return to the editor.            |   panel pail pain pal    |
                                              |                          |
 If the word is spelled incorrectly,         + Press Ins to select word +
  suggested alternatives are displayed. Use
  Right and Left to select the word you
  want and press Ins to replace the word in
  the document.
