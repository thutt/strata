
------------------------------------ Preview -----------------------------------

Use the Menu (F10) Print Preview options to specify the region of the document
to preview.


          Preview the entire       Preview only the        Preview only the
          document.                current block.          current section.
              +----+                     |                   +---+
        +----------------------------------------------------------------+
        |        Document              Block              Section        |
        +----------------------------------------------------------------+

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
# When you select Block or Section you need to highlight the block(s) or 
  section(s) to preview.  Use the highlighting keys to mark the region and 
  press Enter to proceed with preview.
 
# Previewing blocks or sections will accurately reflect the final printed format
  of the area you select.  However it will not necessarily reflect aspects of 
  the format that are document-dependent such as page breaks.
