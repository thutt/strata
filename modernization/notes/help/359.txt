
------------------------------ Graphics Zoom Option ----------------------------

Use Menu (F10) Zoom in preview to magnify the display of a graphics image to 
fill the screen.  Graphics zoom is available only in full-page preview.


# To zoom an image, position the selector box on the graphics image, 
  press Menu (F10), and select Zoom.  When one of the dimensions of the image
  is considerably larger than the other, press Menu and select Zoom again
  to split the image and magnify both parts.

  If there is more than one graphics image in the selector box, Manuscript 
  zooms the image closest to the center of the selector box.

  Accelerator:     Ctrl Z


# To return to preview, press Menu and select Full-page.

  Accelerator:     Enter
