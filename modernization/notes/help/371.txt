
---------------------------------- Text Indents --------------------------------

Text indents indent subsequent text starting at the cursor position but do 
not place a tab on the ruler or change the block or column format (as do 
hanging indents).  Text following a text indent is indented until an end-of-
line character is encountered.  You can use a text indent on any line in a 
block.

--Procedure---------------------------------------------------------------------

 Position the cursor where you want the indent to begin.
 Press Ctrl G.


Manuscript displays  as the text indent character.  Subsequent text will 
                     be indented from the text indent character.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

# The placement of a text indent in preview and final print may be different
  than its position on the screen if you are using a proportional spaced font.
