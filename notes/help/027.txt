
------------------------------ Common Compose Sequences ------------------------

Use Compose (Alt F1) followed by two characters to create special characters 
(such as nonbreaking spaces and international characters) not otherwise 
available on the keyboard.  The following are examples of special characters 
and the two-character compose sequences that you use to create them.

Character       Description             First Character     Second Character
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
               paragraph                      p                   p
               section                        s                   s
               bullet                         . (period)          . (period)
   L            British pound                  l                   = 
   a            a grave                        a                   `
   e            e circumflex                   e                   ^
   o            o acute                        o                   '
   C            C cedilla                      C                   , (comma)
   +/-            plus/minus                     +                   -
   /            division                       :                   -
   1/2            one-half                       1                   2
               non-breaking space         Space Bar           Space Bar
