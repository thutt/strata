
--------------------------------- Copy -----------------------------------------

Copy (F3) copies highlighted material from the document to a clipboard. You can
then paste the clipboard contents into other areas of the document. When you end
the editing session, the clipboard contents are erased. To use material over 
again or in multiple documents, use Library Copy (Ctrl F3).

Copy highlighted text.              Copy blocks in the current section.
         +---------------+                 +-------+
                    +--------------------------------------+
                    |  Text    Column    Block    Section  |
                    +--------------------------------------+
               +-----------------+                   |
Copy columns in the current block.   Copy sections, including their subsections,
                                     at the same level. (This selection
                                     is available only in Structured mode.)

--Procedure---------------------------------------------------------------------
 Press Copy.
 Select Text, Column, Block, or Section and press Enter.
 Highlight the area to copy.
 Press Enter to copy the selected area to the clipboard.
