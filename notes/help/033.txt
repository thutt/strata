
-------------------------------------- Cut -------------------------------------

Cut (F4) removes highlighted material from the document and places it on a 
clipboard. You can then paste the clipboard contents into other areas of the 
document. When you end the editing session, the clipboard contents are erased.
To use material over again or in multiple documents, use Library Copy (Ctrl F3).

Cut selected text.  (Ctrl X)       Cut blocks in the current section.  (Ctrl K)
         +---------------+                 +-------+
                    +--------------------------------------+
                    |  Text    Column    Block    Section  |
                    +--------------------------------------+
               +------------------+                  |
    Cut columns in the           Cut sections, including subsections, at the
    current block.               same level. (This selection is available only
                                 in Structured mode.)  (Ctrl L)

--Procedure---------------------------------------------------------------------
 Press Cut.
 Select Text, Column, Block, or Section and press Enter.
 Highlight the area to cut.
 Press Enter to cut the highlighted area to the clipboard.
