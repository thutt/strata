
------------------------------------- Border -----------------------------------

Border (Alt F8) is where you specify border lines, gutter space, and shading 
for blocks, columns, and tables.

--Procedure---------------------------------------------------------------------

 Press Border. 
 Highlight the columns and blocks to work with or press Border again
  to highlight adjacent blocks with the same table layout.
 Make your selections.
 Press Ins to use the defined borders.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
# Border lines can be drawn across the top, bottom, left, and right edges of 
  blocks and columns, as well as around the inside edges of a column.
# Borders can be single, double, or wide lines
# The last border assigned takes precedence.
# The weight of border lines can be changed through Menu (F10) Global Options.
# Top and bottom gutters determine the amount of space between the text in
  a block and the border (top or bottom) of that block.          
# Shading is a gray scale value you assign to a highlighted area.
