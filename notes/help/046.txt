
---------------------------------- Border Layout -------------------------------

The Border Layout panel contains the prompts that assign border lines to the
highlighted area.  As you select a border types (None, Single, Double, or Wide)
the graphic image in the middle of the panel illustrates the borders selected.
You can change the border line weight on the Menu (F10) Global Options panel.
