
------------------------------------- Edit -------------------------------------

Edit commands determine whether you are editing the title page, the entire
document or just one section of the document; whether you are working with 
documents in two windows; or whether the document is structured or unstructured.

  document; switch a structured document containing        documents in one
  only one section to an unstructured document.            edit session.
