--------------------------------- H - E - L - P --------------------------------
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Welcome to Lotus Manuscript's on-line help facility.  You can ask for help at
almost any time by pressing Help (F1).
                             
  Each Help screen displays information about the task you were performing 
  just before you pressed Help. You can also select additional topics,
  displayed in bold on the help screens, for more information.

          
Use these keys to select additional help:

  Left and Right move the highlight left or right one topic.
  Home and End move the highlight to the first or last topic.
  Enter displays the help screen for the highlighted topic.
  Backspace displays the last screen viewed. Continue pressing Backspace to 
    go back through the Help screens one at a time.
  Esc returns to where you were before you pressed Help.
  
For more Help, highlight one of the topics below and press Enter.
