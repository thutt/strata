
-------------------------------- Error Messages --------------------------------


-- Equation contains too many terms --

The equation definition contains too many terms. Redefine the equation with 
fewer terms and reprint the document.


-- Equation syntax error -- 

Part of the equation defined is incorrect.  See chapter 10 of the Reference
manual for more information on defining equations.


-- Error encountered during automatic hyphenation -- 

Manuscript was unable to hyphenate the document. Recheck your hyphenation
settings or hyphenation dictionary file and make sure that the hyphenation 
files from the original Manuscript disks are in your Manuscript directory.
