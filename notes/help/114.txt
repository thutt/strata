
-------------------------------- Error Messages --------------------------------

-- Formatted date/time string too long --

The formatted date or time string exceeds the maximum number of characters 
allowed.  Date or time string lengths vary after formatting depending on the
information inserted during formatting.  For example, a date format of
Month dd, year could format as May 1, 1988 or January 28, 1988.  Redefine the
tag format to contain fewer characters and retry the operation.

-- Formatted tag too long (<description>) --

The formatted tag exceeds the maximum number of characters allowed. Tag lengths
vary after formatting depending on the information inserted during formatting.
For example, a page number format of \i\ could format as ii or xxviii.  
Redefine the tag format to contain fewer characters and retry the operation.

-- Function not available in Evaluation Kit -- 

The function you have selected is not available in the Evaluation Software. 
Press any key to continue.  For a complete product demonstration, please 
contact your local dealer.
