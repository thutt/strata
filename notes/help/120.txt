
-------------------------------- Error Messages --------------------------------

-- Invalid marker selection -- 

The marker you selected is not available in the current document location.  See
chapter 8 for information on valid marker locations.

-- Invalid number (<description>) --

The number you entered on a panel or in a mathematical expression is not valid.
Check the Reference manual for legal entries and enter a new number.

-- Invalid page number range --

The page range specified on the Print, Preview, or Compare Document panel is 
invalid. Make sure the first number is less than the second number and that the
pages exist in the document. Reenter the pages to print and retry the operation.

-- Invalid paper bin number -- 

The paper bin number specified on the Page Layout panels is not valid for your
printer.  Change the paper bin number or printer selection and reprint the file.
