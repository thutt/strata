
----------------------------- Setting Tabs on the Ruler ------------------------
The ruler, which is the dotted or dashed line across the top of a format panel,
is where you set tabs. The ruler displays block indents as well as tab settings.

--Procedure---------------------------------------------------------------------

 On a panel that has a ruler, move the cursor to the ruler.
 Press Right and Left to position the cursor where you want a tab. (The Pos
  indicator displays the cursor location.)
 Press Tab to enter a left-aligned tab; ^ to enter a centering tab; Shift Tab 
  to enter a right-aligned tab; . to enter a decimal tab; , to enter a comma 
  tab; # to enter a number tab.
 Press Ins.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
# Adjust the spacing between tabs by positioning the cursor on the ruler and 
  pressing Space Bar, Backspace, or Delete. 
# To delete a tab without moving other tabs, move to the tab character on the 
  ruler and press the same tab key.
# Use the Block Indicator prompt on the Options panel (Menu (F10) Global 
  Options) to display the ruler as the block indicator between blocks.
