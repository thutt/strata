
-------------------------- Setting Paper Size and Bins -------------------------


Paper Size

At the Paper Size prompt on the Global Page Layout panel, select the paper size
for printing your document. Several choices are typically available, including
a custom option, depending on the printer you have specified on the Setup panel.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Paper Bin

At the Paper Bin prompt on the Page Layout panels, select the paper bin you 
want the printer to use when printing your document. You can specify different
bins for different pages of a document. For example, you can print the first 
page on letterhead and the remaining pages on plain paper.

If you specify a bin that doesn't exist, the printer uses bin #1.  If you have
only one bin, enter 1.
