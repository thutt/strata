
--------------------------------- Page Margins ---------------------------------



Margin settings on the Page Layout panels determine the print area on the page.


# The print area is calculated from the page margins and the paper size
  you select. All printed text, including headers and footers, notes, and 
  headlines, are printed within this area. 


# Unless you specify otherwise when entering the margin sizes, the margins
  are in the units of measurement specified on the Options panel.


# Although you can select different page margins for some pages of a document,
  the total left to right margin width must be the same for the text pages
  (First Page, Even Page, and Odd Page). You can create different margins
  for the text area locally with block indents.
