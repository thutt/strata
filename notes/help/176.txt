
--------------------------------- Window Switch --------------------------------

Use Menu (F10) Edit Window Switch to move between two windows.

Accelerator:      Ctrl W

  # Before Window Switch                    # After Window Switch       
    (cursor in top window).                   (cursor in bottom window).

    ..........................                ..........................
    .^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^                .^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
    . 3.4 Management Position                 . 3.4 Management Position 
    .                                         .
    .     The#initial reaction                .     The initial reaction
    .     merger proposal was                 .     merger proposal was
    ._________________________                ._________________________
    . Schedule of Events in th                . Schedule of Events in th
    .   Permissions and licens                .   Permissions#and licen
    .     On-site drilling lic                .     On-site drilling lic
    ..........................                ..........................

The cursor always returns to its last position in a window.
