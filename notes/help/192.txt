
------------------------------------ Compose -----------------------------------

Use Compose (Alt F1) to display and print special characters, including tab fill
characters, foreign letters, and currency symbols.  For example E, c, u, and L.

--Procedure---------------------------------------------------------------------

 Press Compose.
 Type a two-character compose sequence.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Some monitors or printers cannot display or print some compose characters. In
  such cases, Manuscript displays or prints a best-guess "fallback" character 
  or characters in its place.  Also, it is possible that your screen display
  and printed output may look different for some compose characters depending
  on your system's capabilities.
