
--------------------- Unstructured and Structured Documents --------------------

To accommodate a wide range of documents, you can have unstructured or
structured documents.

Unstructured: An unstructured document is created in unstructured edit and
              has one or more text blocks or columns; it does not have 
              sections, levels, or headline blocks. 

              Use unstructured edit to create simple documents such as memos,
              letters, or notes.

Structured:   A structured document also has one or more blocks or columns, 
              but they are organized into sections and levels. The first 
              block of each section is a headline block, which is used as 
              a section title, in the table of contents, and when collapsing 
              sections to outline or reorganize a document.

              Use structured edit to create more complex documents, such as
              technical papers, or for any document that needs sophisticated 
              formatting.
