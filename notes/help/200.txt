-------------------------------- Border Options --------------------------------

The following border options can be specified on the Border Layout panel:

All:               Assign a border to all sides of the area.

Outside:           Assign a border to the outside (top, bottom, left, and
                   right sides) of the area.

Top/Bottom:        Assign a border to the corresponding side of the area.
Left/Right

Inside:            Assign a border to all inside sides. Any area that 
                   has more than one column or one block has inside sides.

Top/Bottom Gutter: Determine the amount of space between the text and the 
                   top or bottom of each row in the table. 

Connect Borders?   Yes connects the borders of all highlighted blocks and 
                   columns. Connected blocks have common borders.

Shading            Assign a gray scale value for background shading.
# You can change the weight of the printed borders on the Options panel.
