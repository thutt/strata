
---------------------------- Typeface List Settings ----------------------------

Font #:    Displays the number of the font (1 - 20).

Font Name: Enter up to 15 characters to describe the font. For example, use 
           Notes for your note font.

Typeface:  Press Space Bar to display a list of typefaces.  Move the highlight
           to your selection and press Enter to accept the selection. The
           typefaces available vary by printer.

Size:      Press Space Bar to display a list of point sizes/pitches.  Move the
           highlight to your selection or enter a custom size.  Press Enter to 
           accept your selection.  (Point sizes vary by printer.)

Printer:   Displays the printer specified for the current document.  To change 
           the document printer, press Space Bar, move the highlight to your 
           choice, and press Enter to accept the selection.  Changing the 
           document printer replaces the old typeface list with a typeface list
           for the new printer.  Use this setting to save a specific printer and
           typeface list with your document, no matter what printers are defined
           in Setup.
