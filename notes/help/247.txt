--------------------------------- Preview Keys ---------------------------------

All preview screens:
  Enter                     Proceeds to the next page of the document.
  Menu (F10)                Displays the preview command menu.
  Ctrl Q                    Exits Preview.

Full page preview screen:
  Gray +, Gray -            Increases/decreases the size of the selector box and
                            decreases/increases the size of text in the 
                            magnifier window.
  Up, Down, Right, Left     Moves the selector box in the direction of the 
                            arrow key.
  Shift Up, Shift Down      Moves the selector box one pixel in the direction
  Shift Right, Shift Left   of the arrow key.
  Home, End                 Moves the selector box to the top left or bottom
                            right of the page.
  Ctrl E                    Toggle between page display and Error Log.

Content preview screen:
  Up, Down                  Scroll the page up or down one line.
  PgUp, PgDn                Scroll the page approximately half of a screen.
  Ctrl E                    Toggle between page display and Error Log.
