
------------------------------------- Math -------------------------------------
Use Math (Ctrl F2) to calculate a numeric expression in a document.

# The result of the calculation is stored on the clipboard so you can Paste (F5)
  it into your document. Remember to paste the result before cutting or copying
  anything else to the clipboard.

# The numbers to calculate can be in one or more columns or blocks. If you 
  highlight more than one column or block the result is calculated from left 
  to right and from top to bottom.

# A math expression can include numbers, operators, parentheses, and special
  characters.  All other characters are ignored.  If an operator is not 
  specified, Manuscript adds the numbers.
   Numbers can be up to 100 digits.
   Operators, in order of precedence, are parentheses (), 
    exponentiation ^, negation - , multiplication and division * / , and
    addition and substraction + - .
   Special characters can include a . (period) or , (comma) as a decimal 
    separator. You must use the symbol selected as the decimal separator in 
    Setup, % to divide the preceding number by 100, e or E to define a scienti-
    fic notation exponent, and + or - to define a positive or negative number.
