
--------------------------------- Counter Format -------------------------------

Use Counter Format to define up to nine different counter styles to use in your
document with the Counter marker.

---Procedure--------------------------------------------------------------------

 Press Menu (F10) and select Global      +----------- Counter Format --------+
  Marker.                                 |                                   |
 Press Space Bar at the Counter Format   | Counter  Description        Tag   |
  prompt.                                 |                                   |
 Enter a description of the counter      |   C1)    Numeric            \1\.  |
  you are defining.                       |   C2)    Alphabetic         \A\.  |
 Press Right to move to the Tag prompt   |   C3)                             |
  and type the style of tag you want.     |    :                              |
 Press Ins to accept your settings.      +- Press Ins Use Displayed Settings +

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
# There are two pre-defined counter tags.  Numeric prints counters as 1. , 
  2. , 3. , etc.  Alphabetic prints counters as A. , B. , C. , etc.  You can
  change either of these tags at any time.
