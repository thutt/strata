
------------------------------- Hyphenation Settings ---------------------------


Minimum, Maximum, and   Specify the minimum, maximum, and preferred width of a
Preferred Space Widths: blank space to use when final printing the document.
                        Manuscript will adjust the width of a blank space within
                        these values to minimize the need for hyphenation.  Use
                        values from 0 to 999, with 100 representing the size
                        of the font's space character.


Adjust Maximum in       Select Yes to allow Manuscript to adjust the maximum
Narrow Columns?         space width in areas of the document that are narrower
                        than the entire document (for example, in a column, 
                        a winding column, or an indented block).  When Yes is
                        selected, Manuscript increases the maximum space width
                        as the width of the portion of the document being 
                        printed decreases to help avoid excessive hyphenation
                        in narrow areas of the document.  The space width will 
                        never exceed 150% of the amount specified at the 
                        Maximum Space Width prompt.
