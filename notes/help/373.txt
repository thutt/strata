
----------------------- Section and Level Conceptual Overview ------------------

A section is made up of a group of related blocks.  Each section begins with a
headline block that contains the title or main topic for the section.  You can
add as many blocks or text as you need to the section.  The section can also
contain subsections, which have their own headline and text blocks.

Each section has a level that determines its relationship to the other sections
in the document.  Main topics are generally level 1 sections.  A subsection of 
a level 1 section is a level 2 section, a subsection of a level 2 section is a
level 3 section, and so on.

# You use Append (Alt F2) to add sections and subsections.  Sections are 
  separated by double horizontal lines on the edit screen.

# When you work with sections you are working in a structured document.

# You can collapse and expand sections so that you see just the section
  headlines, section headlines of certain levels, or section headlines and
  text of certain levels.  By using collapse and expand, you can quickly view
  an outline of a document and check its organization.
